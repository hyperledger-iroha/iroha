## 2026-08-14 QR and Petal stream decoder hardening



<a id="record-dc8199d36ea7d0f9cfa8cf5eb247299fa1aff2dea0cf8c103fd4f8b53c494859"></a>

- The QR stream assembler now rejects data and parity frames whose declared
  totals, indices, or payload lengths do not match the accepted envelope. This
  prevents an oversized, CRC-valid parity frame from panicking during XOR
  recovery.


<a id="record-8e2acb8e281fdf7915ab5f6c3d5ca4f0d696909567e973092cee3c684620c885"></a>

- Repeated headers for the accepted QR stream preserve assembly progress, and
  headers from other streams no longer replace the active stream. Petal stream
  capacity calculations now account for the configured border thickness, so
  wide-border grids reject payloads that cannot actually fit instead of
  silently dropping trailing bits.


<a id="record-5bf3a66966a71162cb74ee501cb062dcb8e8dd29cc33dfa3452d35fd8ed5bc3b"></a>

- All 7 QR stream and 12 Petal stream unit tests pass through an isolated
  offline lockfile; the repository `Cargo.lock` remains unchanged. The build
  reports eight pre-existing unused-import warnings elsewhere in
  `iroha_data_model`.

## 2026-08-14 Bounded canonical app authentication and routed-read admission



<a id="record-fcea06704e02ad7719c7b47874b9552178f97e304df0710c140aea1e05a5482c"></a>

- Canonical app authentication now bounds raw query, method, percent-encoded
  path, account, nonce, witness, signature, and retained replay inputs before
  source-sized work. Timestamp headers use exact unsigned decimal spelling. Its
  form canonicalizer and message builder use checked exact destinations, exact
  singleton headers, canonical base64, bounded Norito witness decoding, and
  fallible signer ownership. Torii's exported Rust request/witness helpers and
  the client request/witness encoder use the same caps and exact destinations
  as verification. Replay publication is the final admission step, replay keys
  are structural digests, and TTL widening preserves or quarantines prior
  evidence instead of reopening stale nonces.


<a id="record-906e40fdbe62b65a14794f9d9f9e91bdfb364a83bccee5e1cccbe80048e32bd1"></a>

- The bounded ordinary iterable lane remains intentionally narrow: only the
  source-proven `FindPeers` shape is admitted; other world and Kura producers
  remain fail-closed pending their own source adapters.

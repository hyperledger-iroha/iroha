## 2026-08-20 AXT persistent replay, ratchets, and cumulative handle-family budgets



<a id="record-eb4cd9cb271bd4facc1aab61ecd94ba1d7031c166261c4d5b03a59a95428f910"></a>

- Handles, replay keys, and proof-bound remote-spend claims now include the
  exact non-zero asset-registration incarnation. A historical proof therefore
  cannot satisfy a freshly signed handle after same-ID re-registration, while
  unrelated asset lifecycle changes leave other tenants' handles intact.


<a id="record-0e480f82d91065562bfa8f78e23086e241e339a6f79608110ea20738fbfc670f"></a>

- Block admission reads replay and budget entries lazily from the pre-block
  MVCC undo view. Candidate execution therefore cannot double-charge itself,
  while block setup remains proportional to referenced handles instead of all
  historical ledger entries.


<a id="record-f5341802539ba353b78273193472ad94c79a48a0f69797bcac926d67808c8e0e"></a>

- V1 deliberately retains every family record because no audited retirement,
  compaction, or per-issuer resource protocol has been specified yet. The
  permanent authorization generation and asset incarnation irreversibly fence
  policy, lane, dataspace, and same-ID asset lifecycle changes, but storage is
  not reclaimed until a consensus rule proves retirement without resetting an
  allowance. The resulting authenticated state growth is operationally
  visible and remains on the roadmap.

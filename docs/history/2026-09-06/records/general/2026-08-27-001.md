## 2026-08-27 Trigger authorization and callback isolation hardening



<a id="record-1e2722daf62561f3ba99763e503df5b5ba398b445631ab3d53247592a82b2d49"></a>

- Trigger registration now requires the canonical action authority or an exact
  `CanRegisterTrigger` delegation; active alias-domain ownership and
  alias-shaped permission payloads cannot impersonate a domainless account.


<a id="record-4139ee6cef46174ae6c0350e57c1bc5cce15012e56ebcb1a8d8d6400d34555da"></a>

- Failed deterministic pipeline callbacks no longer reject their producing
  block. Each callback has an independent WSV and execution-witness rollback
  boundary, transaction-local staking diagnostics are discarded on rollback,
  failed proof-dedup state is restored, consumed gas carries across sibling
  callbacks, and the failing trigger is deterministically disabled. The
  process-wide STARK verifying-key cache is also bounded under rejected work.


<a id="record-6d04785085b1c058c5bf3d5629abf95d8b63726a6151aee477521bd7810ed4fd"></a>

- Automatic repeat depletion now removes account and role permissions scoped
  to the deleted trigger ID, invalidates permission caches, and preserves the
  repeat budget of a same-ID replacement. Materialized time, pipeline, and data
  matches are revalidated against the exact current incarnation before use;
  explicit calls enforce matching IDs, filters, and authorization at the
  execution boundary. Time and pipeline callbacks registered in the current
  block do not inherit an older incarnation's match, and skipped stale
  pipeline IDs do not publish empty committed fragments or witnesses.

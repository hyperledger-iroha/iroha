## 2026-07-02 Nexus autoscale same-block embedded economic cleanup



<a id="record-e4b747277eb2b301a26fb4d00e7e9d6470a6d4dd799ed8a41eb92153247800d9"></a>

- Extended autoscale scale-in same-block cleanup coverage so public-lane stake
  shares and reward records keyed to a surviving lane are still pruned when
  their embedded record lane is the managed elastic lane being retired. Reward
  claim cursors remain key-owned because they carry no embedded lane, preserving
  surviving-lane claim state while removing forged embedded retired-lane
  economic records from both the block overlay and committed world state.


<a id="record-966864b1bbc7000181c82a220d8ad4a60032a5a089d2b44245ff5212598df55e"></a>

- Extended public-lane validator reset helper coverage so reset cleanup
  terminalizes stale rows owned by either the storage-key lane or embedded
  record lane even when the storage-key validator account and embedded
  validator account disagree. Exact key/record matching still controls live
  lane scope, but lane destruction cleanup now pins that adversarial stale rows
  cannot survive because of validator-account drift.


<a id="record-b75fee43df8e4988040eec55ad9728d178156b46f70a3f59766da74ad1629251"></a>

- Hardened public-lane slashing so validator and stake-share mutations are staged
  until exact-key stake shares can satisfy the full slash. Self stake is debited
  through exact self-owned share rows before delegator rows, preventing malformed
  stake-share records from satisfying a slash or leaving summary/share balances
  divergent after a normal slash.


<a id="record-c43c1e43ebdb1234df372ab71c6281f3734af03a2522924ec94fde5cb8f637de"></a>

- Added config-swap regression coverage proving `set_nexus` lane retirement

  the reset lane while preserving indexes for still-active lanes.


<a id="record-017f2923815e206e3f0afb73f50ad9549c9d435defff15daa2a6edc8e247bdb8"></a>

  unrelated lifecycle plans preserve stale lane state, while the explicit repair
  retire removes stale future-lane DA pin indexes and emergency overrides

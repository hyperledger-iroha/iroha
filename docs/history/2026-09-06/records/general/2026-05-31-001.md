## 2026-05-31 Snapshot-enabled rotating Byzantine 30 TPS soak



<a id="record-5d83ac2ce4c498574ff363ac153f4c4d0c803ea7f7f609726f98280a3ef17900"></a>

- Enabled durable snapshots for the ignored rotating-fault 30 TPS harness only:
  rotating runs now use `snapshot_mode=read_write` with
  `snapshot_create_every_ms=30000`, while non-rotating 30 TPS runs keep
  snapshots disabled. Restart samples now record snapshot bundle presence
  before shutdown, after shutdown, and after Torii/status recovery. The final
  strict post-heal progress gate also accepts the completed target state, so a
  peer healed after all transactions are already approved does not fail on an
  impossible `> target_approved` progress requirement.

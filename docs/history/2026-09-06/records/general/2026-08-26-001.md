## 2026-08-26 Hijiri correctness and wire-boundary hardening



<a id="record-d9d0478945b81bcc265a626140ec38d634bb5963c8d57cb9d1365067e0296852"></a>

- Fixed Q16 display overflow and rounding. Before the node-local Hijiri
  configuration authority was retired, its decimal parser and nested-field
  validation were also hardened; that parser was subsequently removed when
  fee policy moved to on-chain parameters.


<a id="record-50eb67eb47a53a2128d40d560e9fce471db4a3569f69bc1964012986f41e0074"></a>

- Positive attestation boosts are monotonic, evidence bundles and fee policies
  revalidate public nested values, and malformed in-memory fee policies now
  fail closed at evaluation instead of discounting a base fee.


<a id="record-756755b09ecf7f588937bb0193ccdefda9547b9e32e12c0f99ac70fc4cbee417"></a>

- Invariant-bearing incentives, evidence commitments/bundles, multiplier bands,
  and fee policies now decode through private Norito wire types and checked
  constructors. Regressions cover valid round trips and rejection of forged
  zero incentives, duplicate evidence paths, and below-one multipliers.

## 2026-08-26 Network time correctness and admission hardening



<a id="record-5d7b07e5220df002154b156ed5ef9058e9d0aa870beec9736ee7e0aaaf53415d"></a>

- Reworked NTS sampling around bounded, expiring probes, monotonic freshness,
  validated four-timestamp measurements, cumulative RTT telemetry, and
  deterministic event-driven smoothing. One live probe per peer preserves the
  full RTT reply window, packet-receive instants own freshness, mass expiry work
  is coalesced, and late or reordered replies cannot rewind the smoothed clock.


<a id="record-3a89fe6bbfaed43a8d8a65b2f3126c0fc518d18bb5f9629b5436816a78fd75a2"></a>

- NTS now samples the key-ACL-filtered configured logical topology, including peers
  reached through Spoke relays, while excluding authenticated observers that are
  not configured validators. Peer selection is bounded and rotating; effective
  topology/key-ACL changes create a linearized membership epoch that invalidates
  retained samples and in-flight probes, and freshness covers a complete sweep.


<a id="record-446f4cfdacb332239c7c844a4bfad61a26a98e9a8b14241585bf16f72b595a7b"></a>

- Sampler startup now uses a drop-safe singleton reservation so failed or
  concurrent Strict or Fast daemon starts cannot leak ownership or replace the
  active fallback policy. Dropping either an unpolled or running daemon
  supervision future now cancels its children and releases sampler ownership.
  Clock anchoring brackets the wall-clock sample; public network time is
  process-monotonic across offset/fallback changes, and a retained floor
  recomputes the effective offset and health decision. Zero quorum and unsafe
  sampling parameters are rejected or normalized, and operator snapshots are
  internally consistent.


<a id="record-69022df37155c8a50c84384b97d8ff1ece71d6e01c7b7892f46bb1fca6bfd755"></a>

- Scoped Rust formatting and whitespace checks pass, as does
  `cargo check -p iroha_core --lib`. The two supervisor cancellation
  regressions and four configured-peer sampling regressions pass. The focused
  `iroha_core` test binary remains blocked before the NTS regression can run by
  unrelated existing test-code errors in beacon, executor, privacy-engine,
  trigger, Sumeragi, and state tests; the multi-hour workspace suite was not
  run against that dirty test tree.

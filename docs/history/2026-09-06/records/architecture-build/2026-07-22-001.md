## 2026-07-22 Runaway-memory containment



<a id="record-2c30455139117860f418d281802484ba4eb46ae63d6542475feb7c7d0aadf80b"></a>

- Removed the TLAPM frontend root cause rather than relying on that ceiling.
  The 57,058-line `SumeragiV2AsyncLivenessProofs` root is now a declaration-free
  compatibility façade over 20 bounded proof-bearing shards and one explicit
  three-theorem debt shard. Every proof shard receives a strict frontend-only
  preflight and then a fresh backend process with a unique disposable cache.
  The structural gate enforces the ordered predecessor chain, unique symbol
  providers, no forward family references, 256 KiB/5,500-line/150-theorem
  shard caps, 600-line/256-step theorem caps, and the exact proofless debt
  inventory. Release evidence maps façade ledger symbols to their physical
  shard logs.


<a id="record-b2dd369e2c174baa34be805d8f277aebc2532c25238af4371f2f3bb16a7ccb60"></a>

- Bootstrap payload version 5 now authenticates canonical per-phase
  virtual-region breakpoints captured from the final key-generation circuits.
  Runtime rejects malformed, out-of-domain, or shape-incompatible breakpoint
  layouts and constructs witness-generation-only live builders from the
  authenticated values, avoiding a second retained key-generation constraint
  graph beside the processed proving key.


<a id="record-957bac8189af11ccc8083ff250d5f8feb1c18dc563c67992dc783932456fd4df"></a>

- The consuming proof path now evaluates copy-permutation quotient constraints
  one degree-sized sigma chunk at a time instead of transforming and retaining
  all 534 sigma columns together. It preserves the original Horner order and
  proof bytes while avoiding the complete transient sigma-coset inventory.
  It also defers the instance FFT until its Lagrange form
  can be consumed and releases configure metadata, selector polynomials, and
  the evaluator graph at their last use. Evaluation domains eagerly initialize
  only the base FFT table, leaving unused 2n/4n tables lazy (roughly 24 MiB at
  k16). Quotient parts stream directly into their final interleaved polynomial
  (roughly 8 MiB), and cached recursive FFT scratch is evicted before h-piece
  MSMs (roughly 8 MiB). Parameter construction evicts the one-shot Eq/Ep
  projective FFT caches immediately, removing roughly 12 MiB of ARM k16
  worker-lifetime retention. The outer generation lifecycle remains in a
  disposable one-worker pool; large MSMs alone acquire process-wide admission
  before preprocessing and use a fixed two-worker window pool. This bounds
  concurrent scalar/base copies, bucket tables, and allocator caches without
  changing accumulator order. The checked static admission estimate is
  9,747,562,496 bytes (9.078125 GiB), not a physical-memory prediction. It
  remains below the reviewed 12 GiB exact-profile preflight ceiling and the
  non-raiseable 16 GiB userspace guard.

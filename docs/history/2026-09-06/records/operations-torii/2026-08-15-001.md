## 2026-08-15 Taira manifest validator-binding rollout guard



<a id="record-394f501ea30363e0262f39aa7bc4270020a7f5db991cfb9d1505f8f0011e52ae"></a>

- `/status.teu_lane_commit` now exposes each manifest's validator bindings as
  schema-closed `{validator, peer_id, torii_url}` rows, sorted canonically and
  populated directly from the active governance rules. Older decoded status
  payloads default the appended collection to empty, while absent manifests
  explicitly clear it.


<a id="record-670ec2722187f73d2cee70f8126b782c653368deed8ebb38fdec21292042ae9a"></a>

- The Taira validator renderer now binds each required direct public Torii
  origin into the generated governance manifest and canonicalizes HTTPS
  origins before duplicate detection. The rollout gate requires exact
  validator-roster/binding-account equality, identical complete projections
  from roster-bearing lanes in one dataspace and across advancing samples, and
  rejects partial account, `PeerId`, or canonical Torii-origin reuse across
  dataspaces.


<a id="record-f6d35728eedf6a06278650aeb284def04cc8e15ddf6a1c6deb89968bc93bf59a"></a>

- These checks prove fail-closed declared identities and routes only; they do
  not attest distinct physical machines or replace external deployment
  evidence. No live Taira or Minamoto inspection or mutation was performed.

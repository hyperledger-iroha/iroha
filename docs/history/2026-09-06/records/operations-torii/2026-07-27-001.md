## 2026-07-27 Taira stall, restart latency, and status-height repair



<a id="record-fb665246356d78f132182437b2d658f713043ac6c8ec939294e90bf20cece5f8"></a>

- Restart profiling found four historical block-body walks and three BLS/PoP
  verification passes before a peer became usable. Kura now performs one
  deterministic, fixed-batch parallel finality audit and carries a
  metadata-only, storage-identity-bound authorization through retained-record
  validation, replay planning, and active-height recovery. Any canonical,
  finality, retained, checkpoint, commit-manifest, or lane-sidecar identity
  change fails closed before recovery mutates consensus storage. A guard clears
  the startup-only inventory on every launch success or error, while runtime
  verification retains its fixed 64-entry cache.


<a id="record-01e259eb1797aa7e692596c5491aa8de870792dc2a7cbcb4c29f5098698de5d7"></a>

- `/status.blocks` was sourced from an asynchronous telemetry counter that
  walks missed Kura bodies and publishes only after finishing the batch. The
  legacy CommitQC fallback had also lost its production writer during the v2
  migration, leaving it at zero. Torii now reads the atomic query-visible
  committed state height directly. It uses that value exactly: neither a lazy
  counter that trails during catch-up nor a Kura/CommitQC value ahead of WSV
  apply can make the public field lag or lead state.


<a id="record-e7c4c091cc3ba254ea14ddc6326cf6e3e6155f4066bcc2b6879acab6a7f91449"></a>

- The public edge was moved from the stalled peer to a healthy validator
  without resetting the live chain. Three validators continued one common
  lineage while the fourth recovered from height 9,031 through normal
  authenticated catch-up. The guarded rollout, restart timing, and mandatory
  signed public canary remain to be recorded after deployment.

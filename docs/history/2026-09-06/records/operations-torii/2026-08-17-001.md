## 2026-08-17 explicit Taira receipt signers and lifecycle capture



<a id="record-8d2216dd212082ab9a2a974df1d4a907f05abc863a5afad44fdae73777035e58"></a>

- Every Taira validator now requires a dedicated runtime-only secp256k1 Torii
  submission-receipt key pair. The renderer checks the exact public/private
  pair, rejects malformed or reused keys, injects it only into that validator's
  owner-private config, and derives a domain-separated public node ID. The
  secret-free ordered public map is carried through reset preparation, the
  four-peer receipt, rollout admission, qualification, and deployment.


<a id="record-b06544edbf6131177e5569007ba2eeee67a7e03439a32944023d296a32b610b7"></a>

- The supervisor can now maintain an opt-in durable peer-local lifecycle journal,
  and the offline four-peer collector validates and retains the exact four raw
  chains, preserves their deploy-bound identities, globally resequences their
  records, passes all five journals to one digest-pinned native verifier, and
  publishes only its captured, request-bound receipt with the final lifecycle
  evidence. The lease,
  supervisor, and collector focused corridor is green at 61 tests; the public
  evidence and authority suites are green at 126
  tests. These helpers are not yet installed controller operations and are not
  release authority.

## 2026-08-15 Sumeragi stale-state and deadline hardening



<a id="record-dd816d824213425ba2233136ccbf2859ed0bbb5f1a8a46846614372cd285ac69"></a>

- PrepareQC admission now discards certificates below the durable high QC and
  prunes volatile certificate caches after persistence to the current live
  body owner plus the durable high/lock references. Authenticated historical
  replay can no longer grow those caches with the number of past views.


<a id="record-ec23a686570efa42ebb16d061e447badb8670a81c11e011c60c10211d9ab36db"></a>

- A same-view generation upgrade now evicts timeout lifecycle owners from the
  prior generation, so the upgraded deadline mints an exact owner instead of
  failing closed. Consensus penalty eligibility also uses checked height
  addition, preventing an overflowing positive delay from becoming immediately
  slashable at `u64::MAX`.


<a id="record-32d60a81e068f4e1a22ffe120c8ab0e35388ed23360a0338bd72553c9b6502dc"></a>

- Restart recovery now supersedes a newer undispatched retransmit when the
  durable timeout owns the earlier actor ordinal, avoiding a deadline
  fail-close after a retained Serve barrier. Test-only scheduler fixtures no
  longer persist synthetic WAL identities into production-shaped ledgers.


<a id="record-8622c6b895727f52ede43a076ff824217aace2aeaf4289d90e78b1b1ea581327"></a>

- Recovered lifecycle-owner startup now keeps reconstruction, fsync, and Sign
  installation authority behind consuming heap stages. The phase-vote,
  no-vote, and Decision-Fetch branches pass on the ordinary libtest stack, and
  exact recovered-Prepare joins re-enable status publication only after the
  complete storage/registry open succeeds.


<a id="record-f9910d9314517fec128ac2d35da53498f9964e53d9a31013abec2cd3309faf6b"></a>

- A durable local timeout now fences current-round Prepare and candidate body
  acquisition identically in live and replay state. Late PrepareQCs remain
  durable retransmittable control evidence, while periodic recovery skips their
  closed-view body owner and falls through to the protected durable lock.


<a id="record-75cd669ff57eaf1965b40e4773c1b9e43219d9c94908c2a4047a380c4c43b0d4"></a>

- Focused reducer, runtime timeout/cache, persistence/restart, status-ordering,
  and source-seal regressions pass. The current serialized V2-core lib-test
  binary passes all 106 tests after its certified-fetch assertions were aligned
  with complete frozen-roster archive sourcing and proposal-scoped Set B
  fallback.

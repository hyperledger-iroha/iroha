## 2026-08-04 Sumeragi v2 exact liveness ownership and restart retirement



<a id="record-c1ff28c55820403f2136fa9d81d5a9c6cab285279a523d0c192e59af359aac80"></a>

- The revision-4 runtime now accounts Proposal, Normal, Progress, Completion,
  and the reserved `BodyAvailable` handoff as one exact bounded ingress
  geometry. Reserving a body completion is atomic with conflicting Proposal
  retirement, exact retries coalesce without spending another position, and
  TC, durable-signing, Decision, and certified-view preemption retire only the
  unprotected owner they make unreachable. A certified `EnterView` rebinds an
  unpublished body token to the installed incarnation without changing its
  physical admission ordinal or immutable lifecycle identity.


<a id="record-4878251dc35290771abd0257bb902f232c8aeda74f80afe4dd0911f51ebe0533"></a>

- Restart-restored stage-7 `BodyAvailable` work keeps its durable logical
  identity while reacquiring exactly one fresh volatile completion position.
  Reducer service installs the terminal process record, persists durable
  removal, and only then clears handoff and restart-dormant metadata, restoring
  the complete prior alias set if persistence fails. Terminal preemption of a
  materialized token uses its exact lifecycle tuple; a pre-reservation
  `FetchBody` instead proves its fresh effect owner and lets the adapter resolve
  the old stage-7 parent by exact round/subject coordinates and, when present,
  complete manifest identity. Both paths persist removal before volatile
  release, so success cannot resurrect on a second restart and failure loses no
  work.


<a id="record-588b7216a9744669c7b12105afc83ee6b5470a453ed45dc36b7b0d3d04b4b17c"></a>

- Candidate/body admission binds the exact consensus coordinates and manifest;
  strict-frontier changes cannot dispatch fresh signing before their leading
  `EnterView`, and drained exact work remains protected by its durable
  tombstone. Restart-only frontier reconciliation prunes obsolete Reserved
  producers while preserving the exact protected lock pipeline. Body rebind
  preserves the sole persistent producer root, and a WAL-durable monotone
  leader-wire recovery cut retires obsolete carrierless Dormant owners on live
  `EnterView` or durable Decision without resetting ordinal high-water marks.
  The proof-ledger source contract and revision-4 specification bind these
  identity, persist-before-release, rollback, and non-resurrection obligations.


<a id="record-e5b73b3cb7fa4a6c4b462cc2ce8830153af1fbb83e74767ba509e3604200b292"></a>

- Focused `iroha_core` regressions for exact ready-body backpressure,
  unpublished-token rebind/retry/retirement, TC retirement, and persistent
  stage-7 terminal retirement pass. The expanded Busy-batch, pre-reservation
  Fetch, restart-frontier, persistent-root rebind, and live leader-wire cuts are
  implemented; their consolidated focused rerun, refreshed source seals,
  formal gates, and 4/7/10/13-validator unstable-network matrix remain in

<a id="record-7992b108f7f0822fe15329094b12d3ab887efd4bb8e268f1dac2c0f53ef89568"></a>

- A production four-validator localnet exposed two additional exact liveness
  failures. Distinct authenticated routes for one PrepareQC could overtake a
  live Busy occurrence and falsely trip the immutable producer-identity fence;
  the adapter now retires only that independently owned outer route after
  proving the original Busy producer and runtime seal remain exact. Separately,
  the durable Decision/view cut had classified body chunks and certified body
  responses as obsolete before they could satisfy a missing decided body; the
  cut now closes only reducer-producing control traffic and leaves transport
  completion to the existing fetch, manifest, request, subject, and roster
  validation.


<a id="record-20714662bc3b2a1b97fca56c78f9144f36ea469f4bdaff3eba02ce40730d51e6"></a>

  both certified-view and durable-Decision body admission. The new tests and
  the existing live/restart producer-identity and recovery-cut negatives pass;
  the wider fault and endurance matrix remains an outstanding release gate.


<a id="record-69192f910cf349600a2c0e66bbb796df883448721d631822100b3376dc9c71df"></a>

- The same four-validator run exposed a peer-2 height-11 validation retry
  failure after Decision durability: one physical `StoreBody`/`ValidateBody`
  task retained its ordinary proposal owner while the authority-sensitive
  candidate index treated the later Commit-authorized carrier as new work.
  The superseded shared validation-task path is now gone. A durable Store
  completion retains its exact local-body or remote-Proposal replay origin and
  settles one move-only lifecycle Validate admission; an exact recovered retry
  reuses the incumbent ordinal, while coordinate, owner, or commitment drift
  returns the same owner and fails closed. Focused regressions are
  `owner_settlement_rebinds_a_recovered_validate_at_the_same_ordinal` and
  `owner_settlement_returns_the_exact_retry_without_reminting_an_ordinal`.

  target height 10 after committing height 9. After an apparent replay or
  reinitialization reset the earlier counters, the timeout pacemaker advanced
  through view 167 while the reducer stayed in `awaiting_proposal` /
  `missing_proposal`. The latest sample reports approximately 29.4 hours since
  committed-height progress, eight retained semantic-ingress/equivocation
  records with an oldest age near 90 minutes, and continuing authenticated
  network-ingress turnover while one additional ignored Busy reducer
  transition accrues per timeout view;
  downstream, runtime, effect, candidate, body, Apply, and successor work are

  `commit_time_ms = 73,816,066` for height 9; that field measures elapsed time

  block took approximately 20 hours 30 minutes to finalize. This is direct
  evidence of severe historical finality failure. Combining the snapshot's
  observation time, uptime, commit time, and commit timestamp places height-9
  creation 7,999 milliseconds after genesis and its commit 20 hours 30 minutes

<a id="record-a8d303e6fd14e53c9ded1aabaa7f9ce5d9f0a7e43cad8e727c36c647eedcccec"></a>

- The public endpoint still cannot prove transaction-inclusion fairness or
  that every validator is responsive, and an empty transaction queue is not a
  reason to manufacture cadence blocks. It does, however, distinguish this
  incident from an idle quiescent chain: timeout certificates and views keep
  advancing, network ingress keeps changing, and proposal scheduling remains
  absent behind a retained selected-Serve prefix. Source analysis found the
  matching rank-preserving replenishment defect. Passive `FetchBody` ownership
  is intentionally excluded from the runnable minimum so a missing body source
  cannot stop the pacemaker. The former persistent predecessor episode could
  seal before an already admitted response turned that same older Fetch into
  runnable `BodyAvailable`, leaving no legal reopening path. The old owner then
  remained unable to drain, the Serve target
  remained selected, and every outer turn continued before local proposal
  scheduling while timeout/Progress service still rotated views. The deployed
  process remains commit
  `e7e1d0190f63406a87c635f76af700e1cd86850f` and predates the local repair. In
  the current tree, runtime directly observes the least runnable prefix before
  the immutable Serve target. The first observation authorizes one checked
  worker aperture even if the runtime census is empty; later observations
  reopen it only while an exact older owner is runnable. A move-only guard
  admits fanout from at most that one owner and closes the aperture on normal,
  error, and unwind paths. The old persistent witness, Ready/Claimed/Complete
  state, and cross-component episode counters are removed. Passive Fetch
  remains nonblocking, retry-unadmitted polling receives only one bounded
  attempt, and the selected Serve target plus final producer handoff retain
  their positions.
  The focused production-shaped late-Fetch and worker reopening regressions are
  implemented alongside the handoff, retransmission, dormant-replay, and
  composed real four-validator TC/EnterView regressions; their current-tree
  execution and digest-refreshed mutation checks remain in progress. The
  complete source-seal, proof, workspace, and network release passes remain
  pending; no live Taira repair is claimed before deployment and an observed
  work-bearing proposal commits.

## 2026-07-19 Sumeragi v2 locked-round and applied-height rollover hardening



<a id="record-03dbda8f6a6c137cc95115404c183daa2d3a9ea674859006fcd0050a13f5643b"></a>

- Authenticated Commit-vote delivery deduplication now follows the lifetime of
  the volatile consumer it protects. An exact vote for the durable active lock
  may cross admission once per reducer generation after a TC resets the vote
  pool, and a vote first seen before `LockAndCommit` persistence may cross once
  after that lock becomes durable. Equivocation fingerprints remain a separate,
  rotation-bounded history. The focused regressions cover pre-lock delivery,
  repeated TC generations, Busy-deferred ownership, and same-generation
  duplicate suppression.


<a id="record-58ecd1ebcfc92acbcb6a24e0f36ea3c299ae0f4ad8dd0b18657f39e98b03d92e"></a>

- The production exact-output corridor is per-target FIFO and round-robin
  across targets and fan-outs. A backpressured peer no longer stops completion
  service or output to a later responsive peer. At local application, the
  all-or-nothing handoff first rechecks every pinned network-message hash and
  accepts only its typed creation-scope claim. Historical CommitQC,
  certified-body, and lane-certificate responses additionally reopen their
  exact Kura finality artifact, canonical body, or certified lane artifact
  independently at handoff and revalidate the response identity. Current-height
  global V2 output binds to the exact finality artifact. Winning lane output requires the
  exact durable Kura certificate and application receipt, while alternate
  vote/QC/certificate proof variants are revalidated and structurally valid
  same-height non-winning lane output is explicitly superseded. Native AMX
  output binds creation scope, embedded round, and message hash; merge shares
  bind scope and share hash. Certified sidecar requests/chunks bind scope,
  requester/responder target roles, transfer identity, and exact payload hash.
  Finalized-sidecar pruning leaves winning data in the committed merge log and
  explicitly supersedes losing pending work before handoff. Manual claims,
  wrong identities, payload substitutions, and otherwise untyped `Exact`
  output remain owned and fail closed. The runner regression for this seam
  starts from synthesized durable finality; it is not a
  QC-to-body-to-store-to-validate-to-apply test.


<a id="record-5e7b2456053bb0af8033bff9a67c96c17453a83d4226bd45fc66735ffe57306a"></a>

- Normal P2P teardown now drains accepted reliable work from the old connection
  generation before joining its dispatch workers. Closed subscribers return
  their actor-side pending reliable backlog to the network owner for FIFO
  replay to a replacement, and progress admission binds the exact canonical
  payload digest rather than its length. Topology updates are checked against
  the bounded reliable-target geometry before mutation. Only a canonically
  identical broadcast retry in the same membership tenure coalesces with an
  existing target child. Distinct payloads and direct/broadcast cross-kind
  collisions retain exact per-target FIFO tickets, while responsive targets
  continue independently; there is no class-wide parent. Explicit topology
  removal cancels only the old broadcast tenure, remove/re-add creates a new
  generation, and direct posts retain their owner.


<a id="record-4283c206e3b07b754c985641d7d3abf72a4744f46ec8c74875fe8df8431c666b"></a>

- These changes close the observed locked-round reset/dedup stall and remove a
  global exact-output head-of-line stall, but they do not establish end-to-end
  transport acknowledgement. A local writer flush does not prove remote
  consumption; already-channelled subscriber items, relay second-hop or final
  application acknowledgement, true reliable-target geometry exhaustion, and
  direct-post ownership after target removal still rely on durable
  retransmission or remain fail-closed. The local parent-residual obstruction
  is removed, but broadcast starvation freedom remains unproved until the
  remaining transport and consumer-refinement obligations are discharged. No
  proof-ledger status is promoted for those seams,
  and `machine_checked_completion` remains false. The final current-tree
  isolated genesis diagnostic passed 1/1 with four voting validators, DA/RBC
  enabled, network skips disabled, one deterministic startup attempt, and all
  peer exits clean; every validator applied genesis and exposed the common
  awaiting-proposal successor height. Libtest completed in 656.79 seconds,
  including daemon binary preparation, with the retained localnet at
  `/tmp/iroha-sumeragi-v2-genesis-final-20260719b/irohad_test_network_04E5W5`.
  This mutable-tree result does not replace the full four-seed PR/32-seed
  release matrices, the distinct-subject PrepareQC run, strict proof
  completion, checkout-bound chaos evidence, or the 24-hour soak.

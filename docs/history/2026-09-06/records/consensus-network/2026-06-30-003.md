<a id="record-5077acc959310a2deb0037052958920109a3c4315a4c9bc6ec898befd5feeb97"></a>

- Hardened the Sumeragi formal coverage checker so exactness helpers,
  transitive exactness helper chains, allowlisted temporal side-conjuncts, and
  allowlisted temporal helper chains reject identifier-free constant relations
  such as `TRUE = TRUE` and `1 \in {1}` as vacuous proof obligations, including
  through one-line `LET`, unary-temporal, and top-level negation wrappers such
  as `[] (TRUE = TRUE)`, `<> (1 \in {1})`, and `~(TRUE = TRUE)`.


<a id="record-627fdc6afda25eabfcb47387aabfd82b55356b988fced06fe0f83cecef8e2811"></a>

- Kept the guard conservative by allowing relation predicates that mention
  model identifiers, including literal case selectors such as
  `"case" \in tried`, while teaching the relation scanner not to classify
  implication/equivalence arrows as scalar relations.


<a id="record-b783b8767ed62c8831c69383051cb7cb165bb013ef6a125d476a61663e8f560b"></a>

- Added parser, direct exactness, transitive exactness, direct temporal, and
  transitive temporal regressions, and converted synthetic placeholder
  `1 = 1` predicates in the Sumeragi coverage tests to identifier-bearing
  model predicates where they are not the behavior under test.

## 2026-08-26 Sumeragi v2 correctness and resource-bound audit



<a id="record-56c0740ee5f678428173268645e458330bbe257fa6937543941fba8577913e78"></a>

- Fixed eight consensus/transport lifecycle defects: oversized orphan-chunk
  signatures, WAL records after Decision, proposal deduplication across lock
  upgrades, empty timeout groups, productive orphan-chunk admission before an
  exact Fetch, count-only historical response caching, certified-Fetch
  capacity release across view cleanup, and unbounded per-height durable body
  retention.


<a id="record-2c23d90e16fd6191a3e4c225cb5b15259b6bc1139b9e5ce3da594599e00f4b66"></a>

- The durable body store now enforces bounded entry and aggregate frame-byte
  inventories before decode or write, preserves exact-duplicate idempotence at
  capacity, removes incomplete atomic-write temporaries on restart, and fails
  closed on corrupt final frames. `[sumeragi.storage]` exposes a node-local
  per-height byte budget with a one-maximum-frame minimum; the local resource
  budget is intentionally excluded from the consensus fingerprint.


<a id="record-623d1581bd7f91952a63983aa556585b7f5c166974705ab6464e4d681b5862e5"></a>

- Focused evidence is green: the Sumeragi formal unit harness passed 187/187,
  model replay passed 8/8, and the fast-network suite passed; all 39 body-store
  tests, both configuration regressions, lifecycle view-change/capacity,
  productive-chunk handoff, historical cache byte-cap, startup ordering, and
  source-order contract regressions passed in an isolated target. Scoped Rust
  formatting and whitespace checks pass. The multi-hour workspace suite was
  not rerun; unrelated existing configuration/gas-test source defects required
  validation-only fixes in the detached test worktree, and no lockfile changed.

- Historical retained/lane/merge-carrier repairs, reservation reconstruction, full finality and
  manifest inventories, recursive disk accounting, and the local Kura capacity cap are deferred
  until a Strict restart. Total disk-usage readers return an explicit unavailable error in Fast
  mode rather than quietly triggering the skipped full-tree scan. Fast also skips disabled
  replica-advert and pending-control limit validation and avoids allocating/encoding the
  worst-case Native-AMX prune journal merely to size a writer that is never started.


<a id="record-31fb6ab4e1a2a0007e382e47f82810503cabfa8ded95e083dda065744b6ed430"></a>

- Fast does not launch Sumeragi or transaction-gossip workers at all. It registers one P2P
  subscriber for peer gossip, trust gossip, and health only; consensus, control, block-sync,
  payload, chunk, and transaction-gossip topics allocate no subscriber, retained-dispatcher, or
  relay-worker queues. Inert handles terminally retire any missed internal consensus ingress while
  startup skips threshold-signer readiness, height-one finality, committed-cadence,
  genesis-manifest and fresh-genesis staging audits plus recent pipeline-sidecar diagnostics.
  Strict still owns all of those checks before production resumes.

## 2026-08-31 Sumeragi first-release audit and hard cut



<a id="record-e1c840c3e889fe1d5e1cec37c3e7169ceaaff9f53900268f395b51fb4f7a689d"></a>

- Consensus keys now have one first-release schema: role, public key, proof of
  possession, lifecycle bounds, replacement link, and state. Hardware-specific
  bindings and Sumeragi hardware-signer policy fields are absent from the data
  model, configuration, genesis, status, instruction, and OpenAPI surfaces;
  unknown fields fail closed. Default Torii ingress profiles use that same
  provider-neutral model and impose no hardware-backed custody requirement.


<a id="record-c5af5ebf35ec31a120126b5f0c3962f87c8cb946e0a5aa561b81f08a792c48fa"></a>

- Historical body serving runs in a dedicated bounded worker rather than the
  consensus actor. Its queue, retained cache, outstanding principals, and
  source/requester/global rates are capped; Kura reads, signing, and encoding
  stay off-actor, and responses are admitted only for the exact requested
  source proof and finality context.


<a id="record-ecbaab68f16c21505a3e44d78c6c47eb4071a8ec27774227b26598ed2a06152e"></a>

- Equivocation evidence remains node-local until block admission commits it.
  Pending and committed indexes are bounded deterministically, terminal entries
  are pruned in a stable order, and unresolved evidence is never discarded.


<a id="record-9b96a306aa1bd54939bfb6779fde91cb6c136ddc583f7b2630d2489bf8925943"></a>

- RS16 reconstruction treats contradictory or non-canonical shards as terminal
  poison: buffered shards are cleared, later shards and retransmission retries
  are rejected, and successful reconstruction must reproduce the canonical
  codeword exactly.


<a id="record-d4d899af0d8bf8fbf4ad42e87375603f0ab484cc81c1e8cd2b9ed7401271c3c4"></a>

- Authenticated Kura recovery now mints the descriptor-bound certified-payload
  directory authority at the recovery boundary and transfers that exact
  capability into adapter startup. Emergency-fast startup remains read-only and
  does not materialise or mint the directory.


<a id="record-05150ed261abf4904e880c13c223d681835a4098d4fb1da9cc5919ea774a46b1"></a>

- Focused regression coverage passes 15 historical-serving tests, 17 evidence
  tests, 7 penalty tests, 11 RS16 chunk tests, strict and emergency Kura
  authority tests, both corrupt/replaced payload-directory recovery tests, and
  the 76-case source/formal contract suite.

## 2026-07-18 Sumeragi v2 production transport geometry closure



<a id="record-2ca2e381ebf59f7f07bfd03a1ec3774ae9342c11e03dd4e3f300a7e6ba119942"></a>

- Height activation now derives exact checked bare-Norito ceilings for the
  manifest, maximal timeout-justified proposal, payload completion, certified-
  body and Commit-certificate requests, and `CommitCertificateResponse`. The
  protocol-maximum 31-validator proposal is 1,106,267 bytes and the recommended
  maximum `PayloadChunk`/`CertifiedBodyResponse` envelope is 16,844,237 bytes.
  Recovery uses the actual chain-id length and maximum QC/signature structure;
  control and ordinary ingress take the maximum of every relevant envelope.


<a id="record-bf9eda2f24a497845e869b3d65314c05531669dde6a60966e60763a4c29ff4bb"></a>

- Observer requesters and rotated responders are covered by the protocol-wide
  8,258-byte raw public-key payload ceiling, including the largest accepted
  SM2 distinguishing-identifier envelope. The same ceiling is
  used for direct-relay origin and target geometry. Sizing then includes the
  complete `NetworkMessage` nesting, header-framed P2P `Message::Data`, the
  28-byte AEAD expansion, and the four-byte outbound queue prefix. Both roster
  configuration and ingress open fail closed on overflow or any undersized
  ordinary/completion/aggregate, consensus/control/block-sync, global-frame,
  or outbound-high owner.


<a id="record-eaaffe7f5c50c1271ff7255e8a84011d5cc296e8094083afb5755e99d6e1194d"></a>

- The encrypted P2P frame body now separates its inclusive `u32::MAX` wire
  ceiling from a deterministic 2,147,483,643-byte runtime/configuration
  ceiling. The latter keeps the four-byte prefix plus body within one
  `i32::MAX`-byte buffer on 32-bit and 64-bit hosts. Offline `--check-config`,
  normal daemon validation, and the first network-start operation reject a
  larger global cap before listener binding. The sender replaces the former
  truncating length cast with checked AEAD geometry and a checked `u32`
  conversion before encryption or frame allocation; exact-limit, first-byte-
  over, arithmetic-overflow, startup, and daemon-config negatives cover the
  boundary.


<a id="record-af2d3912912169a8a2cdca3f40d00490eb638b6eef8df3e8edca0e19fc10d1de"></a>

- Prefix-inclusive queue charging now returns a checked optional length, and
  Sumeragi turns `None` into an unconditional capacity error even when the
  configured queue is `usize::MAX`. Actual peer queue counters also reject
  checked-add overflow rather than accepting a saturated sentinel. Outbound
  payloads receive an exact counting serialization pass before their frame
  buffer is materialized, generic AEAD/QUIC paths recheck their real encrypted
  size, and stream readers reserve admitted bodies incrementally after checked
  prefix arithmetic.


<a id="record-2f73f2f26ab367971bb0b6e4db3567cc34ff57542fc9adc10adda6a015c6ee34"></a>

- Defaults are now 17 MiB for global, consensus, and block-sync frame settings,
  2 MiB for control, 128 MiB for each peer's high-priority frame-byte queue,
  and 161 outer-ingress entries. Kagami localnets reject rosters above 31 and
  scale aggregate ingress to `(N + H) * 34 MiB`, with a 204 MiB four-validator
  baseline at `H=2`. Kagami-generated Taira networks accept exact `3f + 1`
  rosters from 4 through 31 validators and apply the same
  `(N + H) * body_source_bytes` isolation rule to that 204/34 MiB baseline.


<a id="record-9c13dad02548367610473b0ce1e7c3f8908ab8d8d10e86eeb80016c5c69ed4a3"></a>

- Source regressions compare checked bare and P2P geometry with real canonical
  encodings and exercise exact and one-byte-short configure/open boundaries.
  No fresh Cargo, strict TLAPS, complete release-runner, 100,000-height chaos,
  or 24-hour Taira-profile result is claimed by this documentation update;
  `machine_checked_completion` remains false.

  the 2026-07-19 section above and its exact-output follow-up supersede it with

  progress-witness module pins data, index, immediate-directory, and bottom-up

  fail closed, and pending alternate QCs/PoPs are validated before
  same-proposal retirement. The historical 202-test inventory grew by two

  TransportCompletion-corridor regressions raise it to 228, explicit inventory

  raises it to 229, and one four-validator exact PrepareQC count-and-power

<a id="record-9e97f1c7eadfaee853795d3700cd7abe5e58602cff722169f5cb366c92a44774"></a>

- Progress-only indexed appends now publish a bounded canonical intent before
  either main file changes. Recovery verifies the exact payload digest and
  bounded index pre/postimage, rolls forward only an exact complete suffix,
  otherwise restores the old pair, and rejects malformed or substituted
  authorities. First-release lane retirement recovers all six fixed progress
  pairs through same-object-checked descriptor namespaces before taking its
  immutable snapshot. Transient open/read/stat/durability failures remain
  retryable, while proven malformed or substituted state is permanent. The new
  crash-phase, digest-mismatch, replacement, legacy-residue, and
  path-substitution regressions are present but still need focused Rust
  execution in a buildable workspace.


<a id="record-a05d5e109e53370b194aed1e2fcebd6f98d070362f5e8933a40c640cccba4874"></a>

- Voting-validator startup now checks the first-release storage-platform
  contract before opening any key-specific signing guard. Linux and macOS are
  admitted; other platforms remain non-voting-observer-only because progress
  promotion, lane-geometry movement, and authenticated archive deletion do not
  yet have one equivalent handle-relative crash-safety implementation there.
  The release corridor remains narrower at Linux x86_64 and macOS arm64, with
  Linux as the published production validator artifact.


<a id="record-14a0b5536a750ad3f47fe09cf6fbfdc15ad6e56aaca46ac83cb7bd0c086d73e8"></a>

- Ordinary pending-work exhaustion is the executor's general retryable effect
  boundary. Certified-request capacity is a separate Fetch-only retry: request
  acquisition preflights both ownership budgets and never partially allocates
  either one, while all non-Fetch request-pressure failures remain fail-closed.
  An exact transport-only `CertifiedBodyResponse` with a still-live matching
  logical request registration may cross retained reducer-effect debt and
  release its request owner; `CommitCertificateResponse` remains
  reducer-ordered. Each serialized adapter macro-step installs at most eight
  effects as one causal FIFO; direct reducer steps share that bound, while the
  largest flattened persistence macro-step is four. The runtime services
  exactly one deferred adapter macro-step per turn before timers or newer
  ingress, after older WAL/signature work reaches its serviceability fence. An unexpected
  `Busy` after that fence is terminal, and terminal readiness requires all
  deferred completion, progress, and ordinary ingress queues to be empty. A
  production-capacity adversarial regression saturates certified-request,
  Normal, and Progress ownership while preserving the Completion reserve, then
  proves that an exact authenticated response with a still-live matching
  logical request registration releases one owner and durable reducer
  retransmission reconstructs the blocked Fetch atomically without fail-close.
  A durable Sign at full capacity selects
  the minimum `(reconstruction class, work id)` Fetch owner—speculative, certified
  non-lock, then locked—while excluding the decided body. A genuinely new
  full-capacity Fetch remains reducer-owned `Missing` debt and is recreated by
  the local retransmit transition, so an unresponsive source cannot block a
  timeout Sign. Decision reconciliation filters every retained retry, and
  local-proposal preflight prevents candidate consumption before its first work
  slot is available. The `EffectDispatch` lane exposes bounded depth and age but
  publishes zero scheduler-skip debt because its head is always attempted
  first; capacity waits retain their body/application/local-control diagnosis.

- Production bootstrap `PATH` construction is now manifest-only. Every
  non-protected runner executable has an absolute digest-pinned source with

  bootstrap-only interruption preserves active logs without signaling the
  runner or publishing external completion.

## 2026-07-21 Sumeragi v2 height-2504 refinement-gate incident



<a id="record-68fa55febff10d0e8bae7be5dffade65760a37b2b231dc76c1279aadacac9589"></a>

- All four validators deliberately failed closed within 26.5 ms after
  committing height 2504, block `2dd1b66a…1f425f`, when the reducer reported
  `reducer transition violated the verified refinement gate`. That message
  identifies the invariant family, but the retained logs do not identify which
  of the corrected contradictions fired. They also do not implicate a specific
  transaction. Repeated missing-commit-certificate warnings remain the
  strongest observed precursor and may have increased late or duplicate work;
  they are not proof of the triggering transition.


<a id="record-fa4317290db4488d572a30fc8307b8ad8c250b7a3a9bc5a0fc962ab9619d9be9"></a>

- The reducer/refinement corrections distinguish a WAL record's intrinsic round
  from the lifecycle owner tag which authorizes its transition. Wrong-height,
  wrong-view, and stale-generation input, including a late `Persisted` or
  `PersistenceFailed` completion, is accepted only as an exact
  zero-boundary, zero-effect stutter. With a current lifecycle tag and a pending
  record, an acknowledgement still requires the exact persistence identifier.
  `InstallTimeout` now projects the subject of its highest Prepare certificate
  when one exists, so the boundary, pending WAL record, and persistence effect
  carry the same subject identity.


<a id="record-56189ac984253dd8ccbc547b7a4918f9024ae6a0802fe685210e54be7b61b4a9"></a>

- The fail-closed progress contradiction is now closed at its serialization
  boundary. If validation of a TC-promoted historical lock finishes after the
  current finality view's timeout is acknowledged, `LockAndCommit` remains
  forbidden in that closed view; the exact durable timeout is retained as a
  recovery witness until the next TC restarts the immutable proposal origin in
  a new open finality round. The source-shared Rust/Verus kernel rejects stale,
  wrong-signer, volatile-only, and non-exact timeout substitutes. Diagnostics
  now report this interval as `TimeoutCertificateMissing` unless an exact
  Commit intent/pool already owns the lock.


<a id="record-55879043b489baece8dcaa80a674d2e23edc36b8af892f68517d7626a85e9c47"></a>

- Proposal origin is enforced as decision identity rather than inferred from
  equal block bytes. Any higher Prepare origin fences historical Commit
  reconstruction, including a same-subject Prepare. Timeout-justified
  proposals structurally reject a carried high PrepareQC; that certificate
  must arrive through standalone TC installation. An alternate TC for the
  immediately preceding round can update an omitted lock only when its selected
  Prepare origin strictly exceeds both the installed highest PrepareQC and the
  lock, without advancing the lifecycle view a second time.


<a id="record-e150d1bca56fdf7c98c08d167bb079fbfdea317e703bddfbc02b14a0a38a61eb"></a>

- A four-validator pre-fix reproduction exposed a separate height-one recovery
  gap: fixed-authority genesis keeps a view-zero block header while its
  consensus Proposal may move to a later view. Ordinary locked-body binding
  remains exact-view; a height-one-only path now accepts only the canonical
  configured genesis bytes with no parent or execution context. The pre-fix
  run timed out at genesis with one Prepare per rotating leader and the other
  validators stuck in body reconstruction; it did not hit the refinement
  fail-stop. The subsequent exact four-validator restart regression passed 1/1
  in 79.82 seconds; all peers shut down gracefully with empty stderr.


<a id="record-2a3abd9afaa2f60662c3e9e3bc8058453024c2af3166a10168bfacac2b063eda"></a>

  liveness out of the immutable admission identity; route retirement changes

  `authenticated_wire_identity` and repeats that comparison after

## 2026-07-21 Sumeragi v2 bounded per-source reply ownership



<a id="record-124bd0b58ef5d42091e0748ed96eaf5ac9e4a19fe7d06e19b660857e5fc5c236"></a>

- Replaced single-route replacement semantics with bounded authenticated-source
  ownership. Each local delivery has an actor-global ordinal distinct from its
  connection tenure; semantic requests deduplicate by canonical identity, while
  every authenticated source retains an independent route attempt. Same-source
  redelivery or reconnect updates only that source's capability. Reconnect
  clears a tenure-bound admission ticket but preserves the immutable payload,
  current item/chunk cursor (including terminal completion), FIFO age, and
  reservations; a newly observed alternate source starts at item zero.
  Completed output never reopens, including a certified-sidecar target whose
  successful writer flush already reached its terminal one-message cursor. A
  later delivery on the same live tenure updates that source's route without
  requeueing a chunk which still awaits writer flush; the exact old-delivery
  receipt remains tenure-valid and alone advances the cursor before the updated
  route serves the next chunk. If that writer closes without `Flushed`, its
  cursor remains on the unacknowledged current item and a replacement tenure
  retries that item through the ordinary same-source update without acquiring
  another reservation. A pending or flushed-but-unapplied attempt remains
  deduplicated, and a terminal route carried beside live sibling sources
  consumes zero output reservation without disappearing from route history.


<a id="record-3468f0e9de4d79cb60971bad570b32fca042edf7f61c275021cba4f1ffb221a9"></a>

- Carried the route set through fair ingress, lane-work coalescing, exact worker
  output, certified-sidecar materialization, runner dispatch, and daemon
  Hold/Release rehydration. Candidate route sets merge atomically, and inactive,
  stale, foreign-owner, retargeted, or otherwise invalid capabilities cannot
  attach a valid sibling. Sidecar gates, sessions, and bytes are charged per
  authenticated source while shared response bytes retain independent chunk
  cursors. The effective `network.max_total_connections` supplies the
  independent exact-output route-set capacity `R`; the fair-ingress
  `sumeragi.queues.authenticated_non_validator_sources` limit supplies `H`, the
  maximum number of simultaneously materialized authenticated non-validator
  lanes. Configuration rejects `H > R`. Each route additionally carries an
  immutable binding to the tenure
  that minted its delivery ordinal, so bounded retired-source tombstone churn
  cannot legitimize an equal-ordinal capability from a different tenure.
  Centralized normal-exit/`Drop` teardown retires every actor-owned tenure and
  cancels only that actor's progress waiters.


<a id="record-b237c124c2599fbefb23e1a4d0aa02d457c46a5492abf6e5e87774abafc85510"></a>

- Linearized certified-sidecar writer completion across prune, reconnect, and
  rate-gate expiry. Each source gate retains a bounded hash-only identity for
  its current chunk after shared bytes leave memory, and every actor-minted
  flush identity carries one clone-shared application claim. Worker handoff
  requires the acknowledgement and admission to share that exact claim rather
  than merely equal ticket/delivery fields. Only after exact source, tenure,
  ticket, request, chunk, cursor, and materialized-byte validation does the
  server consume the claim and advance one source. A losing or duplicate late
  receipt is a no-op and cannot advance a later byte-identical
  rematerialization; a genuine not-yet-applied old-writer flush may still win
  once while a reconnect retries the retained current chunk.


<a id="record-d9cf252b815034a0e3f284c51036693b0ac7407faf83c737fc909ddf3b2f0c78"></a>

- Split fair-ingress ownership into frozen-roster validator and dynamically
  materialized authenticated non-validator lanes. Identityless ingress has no
  capacity partition. The exact minimum is `5N+3H` message owners and
  `(N+H) * body_source_bytes`; with no roster these reduce to `3H` owners and
  `H * body_source_bytes`. Defaults use `H=2`, 161 message owners, 34 MiB per
  source, and 1,122 MiB maximum-roster aggregate body ownership. Kagami, both checked Taira
  configurations, and configuration fixtures now preserve that geometry.
  Semantic duplicate/alternate-route merging precedes the new-lane
  `H` gate, and an authenticated non-validator lane is removed when empty.


<a id="record-cc74803c181dd560484cbf23ea00b1a0c84146179933d9be4e380e73eade67b3"></a>

- Closed the source-ownership ambiguity around the asynchronous model's runner
  deadline without treating a proof ghost as production state.
  `LocalRunnerServiceOwners` is exactly the active responsive voters plus exact

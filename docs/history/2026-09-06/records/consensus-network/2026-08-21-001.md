## 2026-08-21 Sumeragi V2 Validate and replay-authority closure



<a id="record-d821ef9b1167d4245c915ab817f08a81d3386767273300874c5a161726403fd2"></a>

- The production implementation is complete for the lifecycle-owned durable
  Validate scheduler, worker, and completion corridor. One mandatory,
  non-optional effect/pending/replay binding now converges exactly five closed
  admission origins: `LiveWal`, `LocalBody`, `RemoteProposal`,
  `InvalidBodyReport`, and `DirectSigned`. Its sealed completion classifier
  covers all nine Ready outcomes without exposing a generic callback or
  caller-constructed replay authority.


<a id="record-89d52364ab82d4a3f4c31b6f8c45d2c57e09b65a802ef6d1580fc5dd838a0150"></a>

- Missing merge sidecars remain attached to their durable Validate row through
  wait, authenticated wake, and cold reopen. Standalone local-body and remote-
  Proposal Validate rows reopen from disk. Cold non-WAL output recovery
  reauthenticates `Broadcast`, `EquivocationReport`, and `InvalidBodyReport`
  into an owner-held Ready-ordinal census at open and launch. Each move-only
  carrier remains bound to its exact private map key, immutable ledger ordinal,
  owner, and Ready row; a Broadcast with a durable Sign predecessor remains
  exclusively recovered-WAL work, so the two censuses cannot overlap. The
  dormant generic WAL batch/driver and superseded legacy storage-only recovery
  surfaces are removed, so they are not alternate production paths.


<a id="record-dc089f6266d2543e2337a50d4089bfd95ba93c78666b651a940332ca7af4b324"></a>

- The source-contract harness now covers all 50 named cases. Its specialized
  live-WAL inventory permits exactly four sealed replay-authority mints and
  three frame-bound pending-owner mints; the active storage factory remains a
  single production path. Fresh focused, Core, static, formal, and workspace
  receipts are release gates for the frozen source rather than inferred from
  the superseded focused-count snapshot. Current lockfile provenance is
  recorded in the 2026-08-22 entry; no byte-identity claim remains.


<a id="record-f4784df3694d70976ef788b1ec86fe3ad6fd4f4c822b633531a9cec368d7ac7d"></a>

- Recovered lifecycle Sign completion follows the outer
  Completion-before-Runtime order. Stable queued ingress is inert and stays in
  exact FIFO/ownership order while the earlier Sign worker result is previewed;
  fail-closed output, pending effect ownership, scheduler ownership, and
  leader-wire terminal debt still exclude the completion transaction.


<a id="record-7569da6babb52f78e7f1025c885e570e488e75bd39375716026ba7f1ac97b0fe"></a>

- Cold-output settlement is closed over `Empty`, ordering/lease `Deferred`,
  `SourceRetained`, and `Completed`. `SourceRetained` preserves the same
  owner-held carrier and Ready row, returns before any terminal LedgerV1
  successor is staged, bypasses generic Completion classification, and selects
  a timed retry before Producer planning. Only service `Accepted` may fsync the
  exact terminal successor and produce `Completed`.


<a id="record-c43550410b793207fca5aa3fe5bea8486f72780341786329b6f516983a9402cb"></a>

- The feature-isolated authenticated message controller now supports direct
  `PayloadChunk` selectors bound to sender, authenticated route, exact manifest
  hash, and chunk index, plus Proposal-bound deferred Hold selectors. Public
  held descriptors carry the manifest hash and chunk index directly, and the
  client parser covers every drainable kind, including roundless VRF
  commit/reveal descriptors, without admitting VRF exact-round rules. Bounded
  route-specific Proposal evidence resolves rule-before-Proposal,
  Proposal-before-rule, and drain-cutover orderings; acknowledgements may differ
  only by the exact deferred `None`-to-`Some` manifest resolution. Focused
  consensus-message-control validation passes 15/15 in `iroha_test_network`
  and 24/24 in `irohad`, and the grouped `consensus_and_da` integration harness
  compiles. The exact four-validator hold/heal/convergence integration test is
  present, but its current-tree acceptance run remains pending.


<a id="record-595a90e0b7b93974f399504a2e84d624bd5640583cf87330aa5fcd803ae2c527"></a>

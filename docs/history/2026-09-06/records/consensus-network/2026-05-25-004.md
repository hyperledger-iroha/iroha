<a id="record-74f912ac4b4ab162c9852af191f19c10f717d1cebbaf1dd6b2820f93b76189f3"></a>

  and height, while delivered-payload matching additionally requires DELIVER,
  non-invalid state, complete chunks, and a present matching payload hash.


<a id="record-55351521907c901d1e1469704f45485d3f30a55871848b70f6d7e50698473aff"></a>

  missing payload hashes.

  staleness, exact-boundary age is due-but-not-stale, stale entries make the
  next due time zero, and future timestamps saturate to age zero.

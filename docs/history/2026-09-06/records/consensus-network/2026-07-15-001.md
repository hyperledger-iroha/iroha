## 2026-07-15 Sumeragi v2 transport and genesis liveness verification



<a id="record-f1f205ac2c3b087b1aa69b28141fba0e1bc48a23f588d555125866c7a8d9cb18"></a>

- Closed the locked-round reset boundary with generation-scoped exact Commit
  delivery while retaining a separate, rotation-bounded equivocation
  fingerprint history. A durable
  lock now has an executable progress witness after every reducer transition;
  WAL replay after `LockAndCommit` reconstructs its exact pending signature and
  broadcast, and leader rotation retains the validated locked body needed to
  rebuild the old-round Commit quorum.


<a id="record-200565b3161f92824bab44c93a3bac4df1ff7246a3a8acdb6b19be28469e80c8"></a>

- The fresh four-validator genesis reproduction then exposed the complementary
  missing-intent case: a TC can promote a PrepareQC which a local validator did
  not previously observe, leaving the validator locked without a durable Commit
  source. After exact body recovery and validation, that validator now appends
  the historical `LockAndCommit` before signing. The exception requires the
  exact durably installed TC lock and is fenced by any higher durable Prepare
  origin or highest PrepareQC, including a higher same-subject reproposal. WAL
  replay rejects the path without that provenance, after a Decision, or across
  any higher Prepare origin.


<a id="record-e504dedf51325f718732ff6c9725dcda3d937754f0203cd6c2a453c7f57f559a"></a>

- That reconstruction made the old Commit quorum and durable Decision reachable
  in every failed reproduction attempt, exposing a second ownership seam during
  competing-pipeline cleanup. Runtime ingress could retry a body completion
  which the adapter already owned in its Busy-deferred queue, creating two
  serialized owners. Completion ownership now spans both queues and retains the
  full manifest and durable/validated receipts, including validation polarity.
  Only exact evidence coalesces; conflicts or multiple owners fail closed before
  `BodyAvailable` can mutate queued-proposal state. The current nine
  release-gated completion-ownership regressions add installed/destination
  rebind and transactional retirement adversaries to exact coalescing,
  manifest/receipt/polarity conflicts, the real Busy transfer, and cross-queue
  duplicate-owner rejection. The complete ten-module production-liveness
  wrapper is green as recorded above.


<a id="record-8493a6e9830f30863593e423f5e5e43bd57f5ee2416d52e23c9d25ae1fa771fd"></a>

- Bounded the outer consensus ingress by both per-source/aggregate counts and
  canonical-wire bytes. Frozen-roster validator lanes and the shared untrusted
  lane have isolated quotas, each authenticated source retains timeout-vote
  bytes, and Progress slots remain reserved for Commit votes, QCs, TCs, payload
  chunks, certified-body responses, and Commit-certificate responses. A large
  body or one busy source therefore cannot consume another validator's
  timeout, safety, or recovery capacity.


<a id="record-2e0efbe9be9e00bb507f44cdf7aca0bc61bdd75cd5546174f77e7f89b58783ed"></a>

- Closed two schema/ownership gaps at that boundary. Timeout delivery now
  checks the complete canonical envelope record domain before consuming any
  authorization field, and authenticated exact retransmissions coalesce while
  the original candidate remains queued, deferred, causal, or completion-owned.
  The deferred-owner replacement mutation exposes the old lasso and the
  scheduler-wide coalescing model closes it.


<a id="record-9aa4da705b24a32432d3f5719ec807e92474a7b4f8c705fe90b70908a81d679c"></a>

- Made Decision installation a terminal ownership boundary. Before its body
  recovery effect reserves capacity, the executor now cancels every losing
  fetch, store, validation, signature, proposal, candidate, outbound, lane, and
  retransmission owner while preserving only the exact decided body pipeline
  and merge-sidecar evidence. Application installation then drains that last
  volatile pipeline. Focused Decision, same-tag higher-lock, runtime, runner,
  lane, and worker regressions cover both capacity release and late completion
  suppression.


<a id="record-8a23ea0f32f544e8603d338e7cb9a60ba30d26ec129d8ea2194e2d3220ad9120"></a>

- Closed proposal replay's split-durability boundary. Executor startup now
  cross-checks fsynced body/validation markers against the replayed safety WAL
  and restores their exact execution commitments before dispatching a durable
  signing intent. The production restart regression advances by an
  authenticated TC to a nonzero round-robin view, reopens the real WAL and body
  store, restores the outbound proposal, broadcasts it, and schedules the exact
  Prepare vote without returning to view zero.


<a id="record-51553673be3508743385442efd171523f7df18be4d1a074f7217808b35704c1d"></a>

- Closed the remaining production-liveness audit boundaries. Deferred Progress
  capacity is now partitioned into non-displacing locked-Commit, PrepareQC,
  CommitQC, and TC reservations. Durable Decision retransmission reconstructs
  `StoreBody`, `ValidateBody`, or `Apply` from the available, durable, or
  validated stage instead of depending on a fetch-only retry. Candidate work is
  reported only when candidate ownership actually exists, never merely because
  the local validator is leader.


<a id="record-35c5b5989a157afba159304a5cedc4ed94739679c6a0ddeeec499c1537d33a5e"></a>

- Made the watchdog height-wide and cycle-resistant. Its bounded rank advances
  only for a greater semantic stage or a new maximum Prepare/Commit signer
  count or signed power; view, generation, repeated TCs, and reconstruction of
  the same locked Commit pool cannot refresh no-progress. Validation work and a
  durable Apply waiting for merge-sidecar data now retain separate counters, so
  the former classifies as body unavailable and the latter as application
  pending.


<a id="record-8b5b9fdc16c32219890a3ce73b9fb5b78008eebbd6a5d84ed5a4c6ce736bbbfa"></a>

  oldest age, and service debt. The runner now marks successor construction
  `Running` before verified context construction. It defers the successor's
  initial reducer status until adapter/runtime/service startup succeeds, live
  clocks are armed, and ingress is open; only then does it mark the predecessor
  handoff `Complete` and attach `SuccessorHeightActivated` to the successor's
  own height and generation. The marker is adapter-owned and survives later
  ignored/retransmit status publication. Complete-tip and audited-snapshot
  recovery reconstruct the same deferred activation boundary, while exact
  height/context ownership prevents successor effect, completion, and ingress
  overlays from contaminating the predecessor snapshot. Constructor/startup
  failure retains the predecessor's `Running` handoff with `restart_required`
  and no activation claim. A missing proposal is also no longer hidden by
  synthetic candidate work on a leader.

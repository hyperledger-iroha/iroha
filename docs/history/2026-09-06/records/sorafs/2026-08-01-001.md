## 2026-08-01 Governance DAG sealed request replay fencing



<a id="record-47ec243649ca43806120e93346a146b407fd089a3b4e2d11d38bd14aeaeb8010"></a>

- The standard Governance DAG service no longer treats process-local memory as
  the authority for outbound IPFS or signed-head request nonces. Each exact
  scope has its own config-pinned sealed monotonic-CAS slot containing canonical
  nonce-sorted Norito state. The state retains at most 4,096 live entries,
  prunes only expired entries, never evicts live entries, advances a strict
  generation, and requires exact provider requalification and post-CAS
  readback. Concurrent replicas therefore accept one nonce at most once;
  ambiguity, corruption, drift, rollback, or capacity exhaustion fails closed.


<a id="record-6e0cf0ae90c7da83b0aa6042d143e925ba657da6a2d93b00020d552ed4944f2d"></a>

- This does not claim ingress installation or deployment qualification. The
  exact inbound verifier and sealed shared replay receiver are implemented but
  still must be installed in independently administered Kubo/head ingress;
  independently administered signing, sealed-store, failover, rollback,
  public-mirror, and recovery evidence remains open.

## 2026-08-01 SoraFS transparency stock-broker source closure



<a id="record-4ef05f11206395b87b281caa3b88a4694f6efc1fc537f6707f7b13fa2343dcbc"></a>

  stock daemon registry and broker already cover transparency roles 2–6 and
  the directly required Governance DAG anchoring roles 7–10. The shipped
  source projects only sanitized public bindings, enforces exact requested and
  unrequested dependency scope, exposes bounded canonical client/server
  operations, live-requalifies provider identity and policy, and preserves
  CAS readback plus lease/publication fencing.


<a id="record-ff5a49d44b3070f38e253e276a2c1ccac47bad7ca093450be0e38656a736be8f"></a>

  forwards the threshold-PRF, finalized release-anchor, sealed-CAS lease,

  authenticators, and sealed checkpoint store through their existing runtime
  injection boundaries. A focused static source contract now pins the slot
  numbers, projection, scope checks, broker operations and behavioral
  regressions, launcher forwarding, and fail-closed rejection coverage.


<a id="record-550fa498e920e6407efb9f6116ed8fdf9304b250616cab1f26ea85fc9c2f97dc"></a>

## 2026-08-01 SoraFS Governance DAG publisher trust boundary



<a id="record-cfaf9e0b764ceab4b9f426ab2f4ee4d625ef32cead42f95e224b6335ca69779f"></a>

- Removed the generic public transparency source-entry write route and its
  client/CLI canary surface. Trusted in-process producers remain available;
  rollout collectors now require checker-valid pre-collected producer evidence
  with internal route identity, durable provenance digest, verified checkpoint,
  and `generic_public_ingress_absent=true`.


<a id="record-ce86fce251e09b6a1dd4eedf8d1b1547ae060a11cf05e20b1ad53f4ea7366e01"></a>

- Token issuance and privacy source events require the exact
  `sorafs_transparency_source_publisher` role; privacy publish-due requires
  `sorafs_transparency_cycle_publisher`; appeal finance reports and weekly
  rollups require `sorafs_appeal_finance_publisher`. All five writes use
  canonical account signatures and bind the server-verified account plus stable
  origin into durable checkpoint/outbox state and publish-index labels.


<a id="record-1461c4ad97a2a2337511bce5abd556136c18f54fbc2dc6ca1b52f5ba22d3d34b"></a>

- Removed automatic appeal report/settlement copies into the unrelated generic
  transparency queue. Internal checkpoint/outbox encoding advanced for the new
  provenance binding. Caller-supplied public Governance DAG nodes now commit the
  canonical account and exact ingress origin in both node-CID and publisher-
  signature preimages; runtime-index copies are verified against the signed
  node, and publication idempotency includes the authenticated identity.
  Finance outbox and publisher APIs require provenance at the type and restore
  boundaries. Authenticated proof-token and transparency submissions preserve
  matching provenance, while their trusted in-process producers are explicitly
  represented by its absence and remain node-signer attested. Other internally
  derived payloads, including finalized settlement receipts, reject caller
  provenance. This is an intentional first-release schema hard cut with no
  compatibility decoder for pre-change runtime DAGs.


<a id="record-725ce2fa9aea96015655882e8040743068b585e371ba22fc7849314b05bca85e"></a>

- The standalone public mirror requires the runtime index's nullable account
  and origin copies, compares them to the decoded signed node, and emits mirror
  attribution only from that signed source. Unsigned index substitution cannot
  relabel a public block, while internal-producer blocks remain explicitly
  `null`.


<a id="record-36a56b085a7deb6af86e8401a4dba2dd6de4ba1d39f14ab5373b60dcde385072"></a>

- Validation completed: `python3 -m py_compile` for the four affected rollout
  checker/runner modules and their tests; 229 focused transparency and AI
  pre-screen checker/runner tests pass. Focused Cargo validation remains pending
  until the shared Cargo/rustc lane is free.

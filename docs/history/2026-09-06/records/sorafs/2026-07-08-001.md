<a id="record-f03a600993f586a54f312660f899a9b53b66b83f08636db395735267727a3662"></a>

  `--now-unix` review time and validate generated payloads against that clock
  before writing. The builders no longer fall back to `generated_at_unix` as
  their own freshness clock.

  parser wiring, and any restored `args.now_unix or args.generated_at_unix`
  fallback.

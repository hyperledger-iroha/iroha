<!-- Original context: Status / 2026-08-29 Telemetry first-release simplification -->
- Twenty-one additional unproduced SoraFS Prometheus families were removed from
  hedging/billing, repair/slash, registry/replication, brownout, and node-deal
  telemetry. The parallel in-process OpenTelemetry metrics layer was retired
  completely: its feature and dependencies, six global instrument bundles,
  OTLP installers, no-op fallbacks, duplicate call sites, and tests are gone.
  Prometheus is the single application-owned metrics path; deployments may
  translate its scrape or the bounded structured-event stream externally. The
  orphaned SoraFS micropayment sample cache, recorder, and always-empty
  `/status.sorafs_micropayments` schema field were removed with their Torii
  tail projection instead of retaining a compatibility producer. The catalog
  now contains 756 rows, of which 713 are registered; rollout guards,
  alerts, dashboards, and operator material reference only retained runtime-fed
  series, including the exact repair-blocked and stack-limit observations.

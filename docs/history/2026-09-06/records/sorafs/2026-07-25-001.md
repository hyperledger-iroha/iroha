## 2026-07-25 SoraFS provider-ingest finalized-ledger daemon foundation



<a id="record-d23ffc761fb1161cde1609ff8f695462525089b45764976507890d1ceddeaf5d"></a>

- Added an opt-in standard-daemon worker that pages one immutable bounded
  finalized replication-order snapshot, authenticates and ingests provider
  content, constructs an exact fee-quoted completion transaction, and
  reconciles queue submission against committed state. Enabled startup requires
  separately identified, readiness-checked authenticated-source and governed
  completion-signer providers; test, null, file-key, environment-key, or
  identity-substituted adapters fail closed.


<a id="record-0ac5d893554c759b0fb68fd43e89e7fec406e7d8ec658aeebb1eb030d5912710"></a>

- Added a process-lifetime no-follow single-writer checkpoint lock, monotonic
  finalized high-water recovery, bounded claims, retry/dead-letter handling,
  retention-only terminal pruning, atomic completion/cancellation
  reconciliation, payload-free status, and worker-liveness readiness. Blocking
  snapshot, quote, signing, storage, and queue work is isolated from the async
  supervisor, and a stopped or panicked worker cannot retain a ready status.


<a id="record-2faf3e5119973d90e46c785d5e1a9922c958cc7e3b2293bf5e8c110d2ec2cad0"></a>

- `V1-BLOCK-PROVIDER-INGEST-RUNTIME-01` remains open: the reference deployment
  still needs real authenticated multi-provider source transport and
  governance-aware external signer-resolver adapters plus a deployment-owned
  sealed-CAS retention backend. The daemon-owned Kura-authenticated
  provider-indexed archive and bounded committed query for long-lived history
  are implemented locally. The durable completion context now binds the
  governed signer-policy identity, monotonic revision, and exact digest. Exact
  provider-owner and signer-policy
  change/revocation revalidation is implemented around resolution/signing, and
  newer finalized observations invalidate retained prepared or submitted
  completion material across restart; policy rollback, same-revision
  equivocation, identity substitution, and post-revocation reuse fail closed.
  Focused/workspace Rust validation,
  deadline-compliant source/shutdown tests, readiness-probe timeout tests,
  four-validator duplicate-submission recovery, and genuine deployment evidence
  remain pending.

  authorization. `PotrRuntimeSignersV1` now requires an independently

  ML-DSA-65 signatures, and fails closed on outage, revocation, stale or
  substituted policy, identity drift, and policy change during signing.

  rollback and exact-receipt policy substitution are rejected.

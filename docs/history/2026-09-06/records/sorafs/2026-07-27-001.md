## 2026-07-27 SoraFS provider-ingest multi-provider source fencing



<a id="record-ec8f678521d182ac74f871bcc4146397b20b56078c362b337654bfd6ee8bd487"></a>

- Added a bounded production source-pool coordinator over independently
  authenticated provider transports. Construction requires two or more
  distinct non-zero provider identities and distinct production-safe public
  handles, caps both inventory and per-request selection at the V1 assignment
  ceiling, and copies no endpoint, grant, credential, or payload material into
  public pool metadata, configuration, or durable state.
  Finalized source lists must be nonempty, strictly provider-sorted, local-node
  excluding, within the configured pool bound, and completely present in the
  pinned inventory before any source I/O begins.


<a id="record-27d8147b478e2275a33e01d4a4269abef161abf01b75e71934f131866a51f53f"></a>

- Fetches try only that finalized canonical order. Every selected source's
  provider identity, stable handle, and readiness are checked before and after
  fetch; unavailable sources may fail over, while malformed lists, missing
  providers, duplicate identities/handles, test-marked handles, or identity
  drift reject without accepting returned bytes.


<a id="record-e23690fefde49bd3a30dc20ec163120540f27d1b3e983146b45df6065ad528bc"></a>

- The standard `irohad` worker now requires an exact canonical inventory of at
  least two non-local providers. It snapshots that payload-free public
  inventory before readiness probing, compares it again after the probe, and
  requires the same slice on every supervised tick. Missing, reordered,
  substituted, oversized, local, or zero identities stop startup or the worker
  and keep readiness false.


<a id="record-e97357d4f51fa62ce2e4bdf34152dd0a4928a3995b505e6450967181907dda51"></a>

- Six executable static contracts pass and direct edition-2024 formatting and
  whitespace checks cover the touched Rust sources. Five focused Rust
  regressions were added for inventory construction, canonical failover,
  pre-I/O rejection, post-fetch substitution, and daemon inventory stability,
  but remain unexecuted in this explicitly source/static-only slice.
  Completion already carries and atomically revalidates the expected provider
  owner, full signer-policy identity/revision/digest lineage, assignment
  revision, and finalized height/hash anchor. Remaining provider-ingest
  blockers are deployment-owned governance-advert/stream-grant/pinned-HTTPS
  transports and source-qualification pins, a concrete external signer backend,
  a deployed sealed-CAS retention backend, Cargo/network validation, and
  genuine four-validator evidence. The daemon-owned Kura-authenticated
  provider-indexed archive and its bounded committed query are implemented
  locally.

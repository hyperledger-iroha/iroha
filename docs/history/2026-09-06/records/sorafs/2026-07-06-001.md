  `--skip-adoption-check` is rejected unless the caller sets
  `SORAFS_DIRECT_MODE_ALLOW_ADOPTION_SKIP=local-diagnostic`. The helper now
  fails before preparing output artifacts when the local-diagnostic approval is
  missing.


<a id="record-9033525a98c930d5de71cb53fda524f0f21c04558597a64c8af5559c8a998b62"></a>

  `scripts/sorafs_direct_mode_smoke.sh` and
  `ci/check_sorafs_orchestrator_adoption.sh`. Forwarded relaxation flags
  (`--allow-single-source` / `--allow-zero-weight`) now fail before fetch or
  fixture work unless `SORAFS_ADOPTION_OVERRIDE_ID` carries a bounded incident
  or governance-approval identifier, and wrappers forward the validated value
  to xtask as `--adoption-override-id`.

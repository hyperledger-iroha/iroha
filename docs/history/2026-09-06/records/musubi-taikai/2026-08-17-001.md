## 2026-08-17 Soracloud BFV base-proof fail-closed boundary



<a id="record-3219b042ceacf8c4e1eae60ba9ea46d81c5f7c7f5908cb3f9a8c0ba28d478aab"></a>

- Core's Soracloud FHE input-admission, public-key, and bootstrap-key proof
  paths now preserve canonical envelope, governed verifier-record,
  verifier-key, and native AIR metadata preflight, then reject binding-only
  STARK AIR. Input admission remains closed until a dedicated ciphertext
  witness AIR proves the exact-residual or bounded-noise claim; public-key
  admission requires a key-generation witness AIR; and bootstrap-key admission
  requires a zero-refresh witness AIR.


<a id="record-3a68401270209593923299e4ef10d27399a0465a18cb14cced3a5598473f70d6"></a>

- The former positive binding-AIR fixtures are explicit rejection fixtures.
  `RunSoracloudFheJob` must not emit output state, append an `FheJobRun` audit
  event, stage an internal event, or consume proof-verification quota when one
  of these binding-only proofs reaches the corresponding gate. Input admission
  likewise persists neither the FHE row nor its claimed public bound metadata.
  Deterministic exact and bounded-noise evaluator tests remain the
  arithmetic-coverage seam while proof-backed ledger execution is closed.

  guard files and 24 focused
  `iroha_core` tests covering binding-only rejection, generic reserved-circuit
  rejection, metadata drift, evaluator arithmetic, native AIR drift, and the
  distinct 48-query Core versus 24-query native full-bootstrap key profiles.
  Earlier status entries that name positive base-proof acceptance or persisted
  `RunSoracloudFheJob` output under binding-only AIR are superseded by this
  fail-closed boundary; they are historical checkpoints, not current behavior.

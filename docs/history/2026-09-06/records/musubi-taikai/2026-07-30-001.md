## 2026-07-30 Soracloud internal identity header isolation



<a id="record-46f51d9c3ff92631b7b3f6261bab94c1ec747fb551f8501ae33aed99a0d67310"></a>

- Soracloud mutation authentication now removes all three internal verified-
  identity headers immediately after splitting the request, before body
  processing or authentication. A client value can therefore never survive a
  failed internal-header serialization; failure leaves the channel absent and
  handlers reject it.


<a id="record-a6e138a67a4f1aa1b0c42af1b69c6ad55909b0ba169006473d4da397fe09f73f"></a>

- The audit's `http`-crate inference was confirmed: `HeaderValue::from_bytes`
  accepts obs-text bytes other than DEL. The defense no longer relies on that
  dependency behavior or on the current account spelling.


<a id="record-bb32bfff1652ac8aef6e9f0143c58db345a5b614310ea3e4fa8d4fb9d9ef2a57"></a>

- Regression coverage injects attacker values into the account, signer, and
  signer-set headers, requires the handler to observe only authenticated
  middleware values, and preserves nonce-replay rejection. Focused Cargo
  validation remains queued for the serialized build lane.

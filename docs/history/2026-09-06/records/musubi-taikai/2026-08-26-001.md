## 2026-08-26 Kaigi lifecycle and projection correctness



<a id="record-431961afc8c318f0964500ba282dd62250fea3b6f621c73edf5f58398d22bf96"></a>

- Kaigi records now enforce their embedded identifiers, freeze every
  session-scoped mutation after end, reject impossible end timestamps and
  usage-counter overflow, and admit initial relay manifests under the same
  registration, configured-allowlist, HPKE-key, and future-expiry rules as
  replacements.


<a id="record-47bdf3ca925758fde410601fc7b3a1af61d3b7e90132256713b650e5f46f29bc"></a>

- Native creation now requires a registered signed host, rejects zero participant
  limits and unsigned third-party billing, and the Initial executor prevents a
  host from squatting another domain's protected Kaigi namespace. All nine
  native instructions remain covered by Initial-executor admission and fee
  classification.


<a id="record-e2a44f2fdd36365ff8cc5b29f126448e56aaecac0c41a024978dae7f82ba0b5d"></a>

- Stored host and transparent-participant authorization follows only a unique,
  explicitly typed SNS account-ID rekey lineage. Live SNS state is preferred,
  while persisted `AccountIdRekey` edges preserve canonical controller
  continuity after the supporting lease expires, is revoked, or is reassigned;
  an `AliasReassignment` edge never transfers authority. Resolution requires
  one registered terminal successor, and branches, cycles, or a registered
  predecessor fail closed. Successful transitions retain historical audit IDs;
  leave removes the exact historical roster entry and its participant metadata.
  Aliasless controller rekeys reject while active call or retained relay state
  would otherwise be stranded.


<a id="record-e92e5e9f3c690617aab9ca7b5071bd2c3247e2a059f251b5499b1912586e2d65"></a>

- Account-ID lineage work is now proportional to the identities involved in the
  operation rather than all persisted history. A derived reverse occurrence
  index drives a targeted traversal capped at 4,096 relevant record
  occurrences; oversized or ambiguous connected components fail closed, while
  unrelated history cannot exhaust a relay-retirement transaction's fixed gas.
  Alias-continuity removal batches all relevant endpoints into one graph,
  applies one aggregate occurrence budget before collection, and inspects each
  connected component once instead of rebuilding the same graph twice per
  edge. Duplicate supporting aliases remain valid until their last edge is
  removed.


<a id="record-92fe528dff09d76b854c0256938a27f88aff19bf28c4044fea312b2cdace6fb1"></a>

- First-release call records now have an immutable 1 MiB JSON ceiling, a 4,096
  effective participant/roster maximum even when `max_participants` is omitted,
  a 4,098-entry nullifier log, and a 4,096-entry private usage log. Retained
  identities are canonical and unique, participant metadata is limited to the
  host or current transparent participants, roster roots must match their
  retained commitments, transparent records cannot carry private artifacts,
  private records cannot carry clear participant IDs, and private segment
  counters match their commitment log. The host remains an implicit member,
  and a present V1 billing account must equal that host. Active/ended status is
  bound to the absence/presence of a non-regressing end timestamp. Validation
  runs before retained JSON is decoded, before persistence, and during snapshot
  rebuild.


<a id="record-6ab7bd93987600629a944d9e8100795e585d80931c3d6964c2fde628af50edcc"></a>

- Snapshot restore now persists authoritative manifest-alias bindings and
  rebuilds skipped reverse account-alias, account-rekey, Kaigi relay, and Kaigi
  account-dependency indexes with both their current state and latest-block undo
  projection. A restart therefore preserves rollback behavior instead of
  silently losing a derived index's reorg journal. Retained call creation and
  end timestamps cannot exceed the latest authenticated block time, including
  an exact-tip hash-only snapshot anchor after bootstrap authentication. A
  rejected post-authentication validation restores the candidate state instead
  of leaving a partially promoted trust root. Future-dated calls therefore
  cannot become unending dependency pins after restore.


<a id="record-4280d03e6d2a41329d20a22f8f96c538440c3ac188a943a2a8be2deafa5f6d0d"></a>

- Production Kaigi privacy paths apply each governed verifier proof-size cap
  before envelope decoding or public-input extraction. Successful host-create,
  host-end, and usage verification now meter every governed gas dimension with
  the action's exact public-input, proof-byte, nullifier, and new-commitment
  counts, and instruction-batch totals saturate at `u64::MAX`. Ledger-visible
  privacy artifacts reject clear alias tags and nonzero issuance timestamps,
  every successful record rewrite scrubs those reserved legacy hints before
  persistence, and retained-state decoding rejects their reappearance.


<a id="record-c70806a8facc0538b875b0254b0ba620ce9692637aab38203cfbfd80afd6602e"></a>

- Existing-call gas now prices each bounded metadata pass as a calibrated
  32 Ki-gas work unit. Record replacement reserves three units for decode,
  deep-copy/scrub, and canonical encode; relay lifecycle and health paths
  reserve two independent reads; manifest admission reserves two reads per hop;
  and account/domain lifecycle guards reserve the indexed read they may perform.
  Create gas additionally scales with the canonical call input. Maximum
  proofless record and eight-hop paths remain below the shipped 1,680,000 block
  limit, while large titles, descriptions, metadata, and retained-state work are
  no longer omitted from admission cost.


<a id="record-0b71d075e5f527004d5e1b7d9a791ebd9a11a0120f3f583d2189c1d03c7a2753"></a>

- Torii signal projection requires the canonical schema, a successful
  transaction, a snapshot-time authorized host/current transparent participant
  lineage, the exact fully qualified call id, and carrier-block time within the
  call lifecycle. A derived Kura index selects at most one canonical fetch
  budget of exact-schema locators without walking unrelated history; every
  locator is rehydrated and revalidated against canonical block or merge
  evidence. Incomplete or poisoned index state fails closed. Cursor pages bind
  the call, timestamp filter, exact exclusive position, and immutable canonical
  prefix, so later appends are stable and a reorg expires the cursor. One
  batched lineage pass resolves the candidate, host, and roster authorities.
  Carrier time drives lifecycle and timestamp filtering, private projections
  redact identity, and every request holds the heavy-query permit even for
  network-bypass callers. Relay diagnostics reject malformed descriptor
  payloads and cross-domain duplicates. Nonempty malformed relay/call SSE kind
  filters remain active empty filters instead of collapsing into wildcard
  streams.


<a id="record-5fa9dcd733f3e8677f12d4adf87ce259d8f86fe9f24d7916c42925e1d08798cb"></a>

- Catalog-derived outer response middleware overwrites `Cache-Control` with
  `private, no-store` for onboarding-token, canonical-account-signature,
  operator-signature, and operator-credential-exchange routes, including
  framework `OPTIONS`, `HEAD`, method-rejection, timeout, panic, and early-auth
  responses, plus explicitly reviewed private route overrides such as Connect
  session creation and status. Generated OpenAPI operations use the same
  combined catalog predicate for every documented response. Relay-health
  summaries carry the relay's owning governance domain separately from the
  originating call domain, keeping cross-domain SSE filtering consistent with
  REST diagnostics and telemetry.


<a id="record-e43a9e81efaec8a65733478980a8faf27dc43da4e4ee5ddc2327d015d98b092e"></a>

- Typed Kaigi response negotiation retains Norito when `Accept` is omitted or
  canonical JSON and Norito have equal explicit preference, while explicit
  wildcards and structured-suffix JSON ranges select JSON. Media types and
  supported parameter names are case-insensitive; JSON accepts only UTF-8 and
  Norito only version 1, unsupported parameters fail closed, and RFC empty list
  members are ignored only when a real media range remains.


<a id="record-8d97e14a2d23d29b70347b1c79332b742e18bf6495f78fcb8edee674835951c7"></a>

  endpoint tests do not contend on a process-relative checkpoint and live-router
  OpenAPI generation can run from an empty read-only current directory without

## 2026-08-26 Taikai evidence, race, and observability follow-up



<a id="record-ffbf62f5eafb96d33ccb01b77550a75e8a4f3b0bbe3a490bf1548b0465b45fa3"></a>

- Taikai routing manifests now reject unsupported versions at the data-model
  validation boundary. RPT verification requires the GAR, CEK receipt, and
  distribution bundle; recomputes all commitments; validates CEK scope; and
  publishes reports atomically. Direct policy inputs use no-follow,
  non-blocking descriptor opens, bounded document reads, stable file identity,
  and before/after metadata checks. Streamed GAR and recursive bundle hashing
  likewise reject content or directory-membership changes during verification.
  CLI output replacement now revalidates the target and its ancestors before
  every rename, preserves an existing file's mode, bounds rollback snapshots,
  and syncs the immediate parent. Rollback is bound to the exact unchanged file
  published by the transaction and refuses to overwrite a concurrent
  replacement. The xtask writer rejects missing or indirect output ancestors
  before staging any side effect.


<a id="record-ec08672dd1264d20ff7140812ee283079240c5b2580b7dfb2cf74d9c4043e8ba"></a>

- Torii holds an operating-system alias lock across TRM validation and lineage
  commit, binds the current head and complete SSM/TRM alias scope, performs
  descriptor-bound bounded artifact reads, and publishes anchor readiness only
  after durable receipt commit. A malformed anchor candidate no longer prevents
  later valid candidates from being considered. An interrupted TRM ingest may
  retry only when the lineage record binds the exact replay coordinates and the
  staged bytes still match; legacy or ambiguous records fail closed, and an
  exact retry does not emit a second alias-rotation event. SSM-bearing Taikai
  requests now require the exact caller manifest and preserve its non-zero
  issuance time after fingerprint derivation, making the canonical manifest and
  SSM stable across different Torii queue seconds. Zero SSM production times
  are rejected. The lane/epoch DA replay nonce remains independent from the
  per-rendition Taikai segment sequence. Completed duplicates now reload a
  bounded, direct pin intent and bind its durable manifest, alias, and unsigned
  authorization digest to the submitted request. Rebinding a stripped SSM/TRM,
  caller manifest, owner, or network identity returns a conflict; missing or
  corrupt identity evidence returns an internal error and cannot publish
  readiness. Equivalent re-signing remains idempotent, and non-Taikai retries
  retain their server-timestamp behavior.


<a id="record-f0bd845c3e86053eea6eee3e364953d95b8f77d1e6d8287df695b1f7e7dc1a1b"></a>

- The viewer scopes same-named streams by event, rejects duplicate logical
  segments, and rejects oversized, indirect, replaced, or concurrently mutated
  envelope/CAR/CEK inputs. Cache promotion no longer reinserts the origin tier
  or emits false insertion telemetry; mixed-size demotions report each inserted
  object's actual bytes, and eviction gossip cannot activate or extend a shard
  lease.


<a id="record-84cbfcfa455ee6879dc14ee6d2fcc2e92665d466bd7bbeadb34f49ad600cfec3"></a>

- Publisher/export helpers preflight aliases, avoid shell-ambiguous command
  rendering and credential-bearing redirects, bound Grafana responses, and
  publish JSON atomically. Taikai dashboards and alerts now use registered
  runtime metric names, correct per-minute units and histogram grouping, and
  remove unsupported cache series.

## 2026-08-26 Taikai durability and verification follow-up



<a id="record-7ed5ec7bdc179f160695329931c3c5cd304166f633c44c6dc295694550150262"></a>

- DA spool batches now treat the durable receipt as a commit barrier: artifact
  actions must all succeed before the receipt and replay cursor commit. Fresh
  replay reservations carry opaque generations, roll back on failure or
  pre-enqueue cancellation, remain owned by an accepted spool job if the HTTP
  future is cancelled, and cannot evict committed replay history. Cancelled
  bounded-queue sends also restore the queue-depth counter.


<a id="record-8e8fa3137188d78f8e2206bdafcaaa1c5c269fc9bde30c60833b19fecb800521"></a>

- Replay windows now bound committed and pending entries independently, evict a
  monotonic sequence prefix, preserve an authoritative stale floor, invalidate
  reservations across priming/floor changes, and expire committed entries at
  the exact TTL boundary. An identical request against a pending reservation is
  reported as `InFlight`; Torii returns a retryable conflict instead of trying
  to load a receipt that cannot exist yet and producing a racy 500.


<a id="record-3dcb98fbcf8dd584c56c6bf35f4fdabc91a51155b8d316b7bf71731e7501a429"></a>

- Taikai CEK/RPT tooling validates typed bindings, non-zero digests/salts,
  canonical labels and validity windows, raw source digests, and framed bundle
  identities while staging outputs transactionally. CAR production/viewing now
  applies canonical envelope/archive verification, bounded raw sections,
  collision- and symlink-aware output checks, transactional core outputs, and
  normalized summary metadata. Cache admission, QoS batching, replay ordering,
  and shard expiry were tightened without changing deterministic wire output.

## 2026-08-26 Taikai correctness and artifact-integrity audit



<a id="record-e9411b2e5a41170461c6d3f3e212a6a705f481ddce46850415313d38216ebf80"></a>

- Taikai producers and consumers now agree on canonical framed Norito segment
  envelopes. Torii rejects zero bitrate/duration metadata, unsupported SSM
  versions, publisher account/key mismatches, and TRM event, stream, rendition,
  global-window, or rendition-window mismatches. Unicode codec/audio-layout
  inputs fail cleanly instead of panicking.


<a id="record-c3b32615d485a13961abe88f20a2874e7d40d7145c7dce45dc47fd57cec8bade"></a>

- CAR rehydration and the viewer canonically reconstruct the single-file CAR
  plan and verify the archive digest, size, CID, chunk root, and chunk count.
  Track/codec shape is validated before writes; artifact paths reject aliases,
  nesting, hard-link input collisions, symlink parents, and non-regular output
  types. In-place rehydration recognizes normalized paths and hard links.


<a id="record-5c314651366ca6b3a40972e2d3114231a7d1ac0f44c9cdcf5b68a9f99d02a258"></a>

- Taikai cache batches honor their projected byte cap and shard assignment,
  pending-byte statistics recover from saturation, and sub-millisecond or
  unsupported-version admission records fail closed. RPT tooling now uses
  canonical Norito framing, checked validity windows, raw GAR/CEK content
  digests, and collision-safe storage-ticket derivation. The ingest smoke
  harness canonicalizes existing path prefixes, rejects traversal labels and
  newline argument injection, and requires non-empty CAR/envelope artifacts.

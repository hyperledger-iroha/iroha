  end of the Kura replay range, instead of fsyncing the query-index journal for

## 2026-05-30 Rebuilt Kura replay and rotating fault soak evidence



<a id="record-80068e32ca914d9f656c600d87bca46827b6f3ebe8eee01d559f209e5c4e9333"></a>

- Rebuilt `target/release/iroha3d` after the replay validation changes and
  reran the ignored 7,200 second rotating Byzantine 30 TPS transfer soak from
  `/tmp/iroha-rotating-byz-7200s-rebuilt-20260530011608`.


<a id="record-c0f5923e75789d9349e66658de8d26a3888da0e09a9eceb8e93555bc3655398b"></a>

- The rebuilt full soak passed with 4 peers, 30 TPS, 60 second rotation,
  `submit_accept_quorum=2`, and the broadened
  `conflicting-ready`, `duplicate-inits`, `drop-validator-chunks` matrix:
  `submitted=216000`, `target_approved=216016`, final
  `min_txs_approved=216016`, `max_txs_rejected=0`,
  `load_elapsed=7200.840563625s`, `submit_elapsed=7199.981030917s`,
  `total_elapsed=7516.591897083s`, and all peers converged at final height
  `1106`.


<a id="record-45ff3211482c09d1a299e032398712eda1f532b5844b268d88e1df07b34c3134"></a>

- The run observed all three configured fault kinds across 40 fault windows and
  the harness-level fault-window overlap check passed. Status samples also
  proved the important distinction: with three reachable peers, 546 samples were
  fully aligned and continued to the final target, while 339 samples had a
  lagging reachable peer during the next fault window. The largest sampled
  reachable-peer skew was 50 blocks / 10,702 approvals, so remaining pauses are
  catch-up-tail and quorum/view-state overlap, not a universal one-peer restart
  halt.


<a id="record-9837c1de0f0e24ce73a6cf81538db429cd3caec606e4d9d860b1525a83b46fda"></a>

- Startup stack samples from restarted peers confirm the replay fix removed the
  old replay-only FASTPQ hotspot: sampled rebuilt replay frames entered
  `replay_blocks_from_kura_range` and ordinary snapshot/index/state-apply work,
  with zero sampled frames in `record_transfer_transcripts`, Poseidon hashing,
  or execution-witness capture.

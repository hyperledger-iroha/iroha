- Privacy NDJSON, SSE, and Norito RPC reads are lazy, incrementally bounded, and
  always close responses. SSE supports CR, LF, and CRLF across chunk boundaries,
  retries only transport dispatch/body failures, never retries application
  callbacks, and checkpoints replay cursors only after callbacks succeed. Norito
  RPC is origin-locked, one-shot, size-bounded, and requires the negotiated
  response media type. Six codec helpers now reuse Norito's table-driven CRC64,
  and the typed package marker plus task-oriented README/public tutorial updates
  document the canonical first-release surface.

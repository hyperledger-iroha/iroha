## 2026-08-29 SORA Parliament shared-budget and release-surface hardening



<a id="record-bcc2be909aa292e6a14047583fff32674f56e91a8e71953d1e06b74a52f19c2c"></a>

- Parliament now carries one persisted, proposal-wide budget of sixteen fresh
  randomness redraws across successor attempts, body-election generations,
  Confirmation Jury draws, and timed-OVN ballot retries. Exact transport
  retransmission remains idempotent and consumes no unit. Restore validates the
  inherited prefix, and a narrow Policy result that would require a draw at the
  ceiling atomically records typed `RandomnessRedrawBudgetExhausted` `NoResult`
  without committing a partial Policy or Confirmation binding.


<a id="record-526d74a4a2c3c68eb3c099327b89d702c20b3b1ac607fb369c85c36185708c40"></a>

- Timed-OVN masked-ballot corpus chunks are permissionless exact-next progress
  transitions. Core derives the committed offset and checks the active ballot,
  exact phase and height, body and predecessor bindings, chunk and record
  widths, capacity, survivor order, and every proof before advancing the
  replay-checkable prefix. Removing the manager gate therefore removes a
  withholding point without adding a caller-selected corpus or plaintext
  fallback.


<a id="record-e0d54d70c14eefc50b25bb7fa7c2fabbf792a5557c0cfed1ce3a1faf327ff747"></a>

- The missing-Validate-sidecar wait now exposes only a sealed pacemaker escape
  for QC, timeout-vote, and timeout-certificate ingress, so consensus can
  advance without admitting ordinary lane work. A certified newer view
  durably supersedes only an unprotected older wait, clears retry ownership,
  and recovers residual post-ledger registration; focused regressions cover
  permission minting, pacemaker service order, protected-wait preservation, and
  crash cleanup.


<a id="record-6325b4afe9e7f58f0e5c60ecd9ac7d91c89fc3bf3e3a87f8e0aa78b9a6e1ca74"></a>

- Contract lifecycle and emergency-hold proposal effects use exact revision
  compare-and-set, owner/delegation authorization, ABI/artifact validation, and
  complete post-state audit events. An expired emergency hold remains retained
  until the append-only lifecycle action
  `CompleteEmergencyHoldRetrospective` (Norito index 5) rebinds the exact hold
  proposal, attempt, and incident digest plus a nonzero finding root. Certified
  enactment alone clears that hold and permits a later independent hold; early,
  zero-root, substituted, missing, and replayed retrospectives fail closed.

<a id="record-8408c62b0def4ee927417137d7b21326704e647edd34c0ceb0d1d090369ddbd7"></a>

- Hidden Policy and Confirmation ballots now share one canonical minimum of
  three configured seats, eligible candidates, sealed assignments, frozen
  survivors, and accepted ballots. A three-member registration that drops to
  two is rejected atomically before survivor evidence, ballot acceptance,
  opening, or an exact tally can persist; the ordinary permissionless deadline
  transition records `SurvivorDeadlineExpired` `NoResult` and preserves the
  bounded fresh-attempt retry path. Reducer, timed-OVN replay, restore, tally,
  configuration, sortition, and focused dropout tests all enforce the same
  floor.


<a id="record-0f5eac4c6ef64cb4b58a5f0874e8da37a30f070c77ce446afd099dae732fa234"></a>

- The research and claim boundary was reviewed through 30 August 2026. V1
  exposes exact tallies, corpus size, and deterministic participant hashes, so
  it provides ballot-value confidentiality with linkable participation—not
  anonymous, winner-only, receipt-free, coercion-resistant, Sybil-resistant,
  post-quantum, or end-to-end voter-verifiable voting. The three-member floor
  eliminates the concrete reachable two-survivor disclosure; it does not stop
  unanimous-tally or auxiliary-knowledge inference and is not a general
  B-privacy, receipt-freeness, or coercion-resistance proof. Session-use limits
  bound exposure but do not detect compromise or provide proactive refresh,
  and the finite model is not a composed cryptographic proof.

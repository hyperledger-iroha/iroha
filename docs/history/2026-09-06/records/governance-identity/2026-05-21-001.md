## 2026-05-21 SORA Parliament equal-citizen alignment (superseded pre-release work)



<a id="record-c5cd1983f39f6f5ac6d4679e91db87b8f79ae41b0dc1c66c3ce011c3dda20c78"></a>

- This pre-release checkpoint established the equal-citizen bond and draw rule,
  but its public stage-ballot, fallback-council, referendum-finalization, and
  client-enactment design was removed before V1.


<a id="record-ca2fe2eed19778536fb8a94bbf6324a3675df443b7d2cc73a037d6a8d15a6d71"></a>

- The current first-release surface is the attempt-based Parliament reducer:
  authority-bound sortition and timed-OVN transitions produce a complete
  certificate, and Core executes its typed effect automatically at the exact due
  height. Persisted governance state must use the fresh-genesis V1 schema;
  legacy roster and council rows are rejected rather than decoded, projected,
  or consulted.

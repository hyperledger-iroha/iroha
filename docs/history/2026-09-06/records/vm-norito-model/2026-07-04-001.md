## 2026-07-04 IVM Ed25519 public-key admission preflight



<a id="record-4b9943740ab856767ef9c2eb60a2a3671092d363eeb89263a75383d7278f24e9"></a>

- Tightened IVM Ed25519 verifier admission so raw public-key bytes pass through
  `iroha_crypto::ed25519_parse_public_key` before any CPU verifier,
  deterministic batch verifier, CUDA/Metal staging path, or Halo2 adapter sees
  them. The shared IVM preflight now rejects all-zero, small-order, malformed,
  and noncanonical public-key encodings, including a noncanonical non-small-order
  point that would not be caught by a weak-key-only check.


<a id="record-6d17402ca5377169a05c8a4d23feb33136bf56a894d74d828654267af1027613"></a>

- Rechecked remaining direct `ed25519_dalek::VerifyingKey::from_bytes` sites:
  non-test production paths outside IVM are either immediately fed from
  `ed25519_parse_public_key`/local canonical helpers or are signature-`R`
  point checks; the remaining direct calls are tests, fixture validation, or
  generated-key assertions.

- The derive-heavy canonical and prefix field decoders now delegate alignment,
  decode-root/flag context, panic suppression, payload accounting, and
  consumption policy to one non-generic engine. Only small typed deserialize,
  serialization-length, and ownership shims remain per field type. The exact
  eight-unit no-run rebuild completed in 366.79 seconds at 12,311,396,352 bytes
  (11.466 GiB) kernel high-water RSS, down 742.2 MiB (5.95%) from the comparable
  16-unit pre-refactor run; the eight-object rlib shrank from 570,253,400 to
  470,810,416 bytes. This materially reduces but does not eliminate the large
  frontend/derive/monomorphization footprint, so further data-model splitting
  remains follow-up work.

## 2026-08-28 Inrou V1 publication liveness and Taira release gate



<a id="record-16089238a66c08fbe71d9b720c33f062a7d59164447dc650c5d4c42a5ec40ba4"></a>

- Inrou has one first-release V1/current-revision path with no compatibility
  alias, fallback decoder, or production toggle. Offline service/application
  preseed durably qualifies the exact local SoraFS bundle, guest, and canonical
  public-discovery artifacts, writes one owner-only canonical Norito receipt,
  and releases every store lock. Online deploy, upgrade, and release accept
  only that receipt and revalidate its artifacts, placement, active validator,
  and peer before the first pin or service mutation. Startup retains the exact
  qualified-manifest allowlist: locally assigned Inrou artifacts cannot fall
  back to a remote provider, while the generic deterministic-IVM path retains
  its existing remote-provider behavior.


<a id="record-eddc1c36f8a2120912134fd9c3b952067520cce17be7e73a01bfa16ede50a206"></a>

- The Taira reset contract now has seven ordered prepared children: onboarding,
  faucet, final canary, bundle pin, guest pin, discovery pin, and service
  mutation. The final canary binds the current and revision discovery routes,
  the canonical path host, and CID host; every pin requires the expected
  approved epoch. Cleanup removes only superseded marker-bound Inrou stage
  roots under root-private custody and preserves current stages, selected and
  rollback releases, secrets, journals, and durable preseed receipts.

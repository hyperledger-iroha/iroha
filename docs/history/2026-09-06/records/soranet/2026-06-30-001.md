## 2026-06-30 SoraNet SRCv2 certificate timestamp sign guard



<a id="record-56453dcb03a9162fb39a7a2510f4f59e8b8a5df68986fdba5f81535926d2fa45"></a>

- Hardened SRCv2 certificate payload admission so `published_at`,
  `valid_after`, and `valid_until` Unix-second fields must be non-negative
  before validity-window ordering checks. This prevents signed relay
  certificates with negative publication or validity timestamps from reaching
  directory window validation or signature verification as otherwise
  well-formed certificate payloads.

## 2026-06-30 SoraNet PoW/Puzzle expiry overflow guard



<a id="record-6d3732ed78edb5c2f646c7d4fe756058c9d97234b3a78cd0133e0d08295fffa0"></a>

- Hardened SoraNet PoW ticket parsing, signed-ticket decoding, PoW
  verification, and Argon2 puzzle verification so direct or decoded tickets
  with `expires_at = u64::MAX` or any other unrepresentable UNIX-second expiry
  return explicit `ExpiryTimestampOverflow` errors before TTL/skew
  classification, challenge verification, Argon2 work, signature preflight,
  public-key validation, or replay-store mutation.

  verification, signed-ticket verification, and Argon2 puzzle verification to

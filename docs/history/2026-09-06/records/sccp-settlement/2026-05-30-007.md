<a id="record-ef30db26059eff1c3360f7a82a35094208917dac4a44fd8f3c6643fc88ca58a0"></a>

  the canonical SORA -> Solana route-canary ProgramData evidence transcript and
  BLAKE2b-256 hash used by core readiness. The helpers validate finalized RPC
  evidence, BPF upgradeable-loader ownership, immutable ProgramData metadata,
  audited slots, executable ELF bytes, ProgramData byte/code hashes, source

  Solana canary around the wrong destination rollout.

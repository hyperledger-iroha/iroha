  commitments, encrypted outputs, replay receipts, and aborts. Global planning

  transaction overlay; invalid or partially valid receipts leave every pool
  head and receipt unchanged. Torii and the WSV mutation boundary both require
  the active compiled IVM private-note capability, the separately compiled
  settlement profile, governed activation/notice heights, and V1 policy.


<a id="record-166022db3d2411fc40ec0ed60074f9d490c4f32f586c94ac90c3f86154b53369"></a>

- Hardened the implemented security boundaries around that state transition.
  Each committee authority must equal the exact ordered four-validator roster
  and lane incarnation resolved from consensus state at
  `authority_context_height`; capsule and per-auditor wrap AAD now bind that
  authority digest and height. Durable staged locks are keyed by the full route
  as well as pool/head, nullifier, or output commitment. Torii measures
  `max_capsule_bytes` over the whole canonical capsule and enforces
  `default_min_auditor_approvals` as a governed policy floor. Coordinator and
  WSV preflight now measure the complete boxed finalization instruction, while
  Torii ingress and the core one-shot carrier binding enforce
  `max_carrier_bytes` over the exact canonical sponsor-signed transaction,
  including authority, metadata, fee intent, and signature. Torii rejects a
  carrier whose authority window has not opened, has no room for the next block
  before expiry, or exceeds the governed expiry span.

- Added the privacy-governance-authorized
  `RotatePrivateSettlementPoolPolicyV1` transition. It preserves the pool
  frontier and replay state, requires exact predecessor/asset bindings, the
  next public revision and a newer key epoch, and rejects a same-height receipt

  pre-rotation receipts valid across restart; an exact terminal replay is
  rejected without mutating any WSV byte. In-flight old-policy bundles fail
  closed. Retired-key custody or governed capsule rewrapping remains an operator
  qualification gate.

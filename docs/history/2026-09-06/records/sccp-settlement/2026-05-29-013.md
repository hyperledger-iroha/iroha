## 2026-05-29 SCCP TON live-evidence response binding



<a id="record-16656e1637d36f54313b2eb1ecbc9309013e0f9a19c2e845ce73fbe4324a392a"></a>

- Hardened the TON live destination evidence collector so TON Center hash
  fields are parsed as exact 32-byte hex when `0x`-prefixed, or strict
  base64/base64url otherwise. Malformed remote hash text can no longer be
  normalized by Python's permissive base64 decoder before verifier code,
  account-state, or last-transaction hashes are pinned into rollout evidence.

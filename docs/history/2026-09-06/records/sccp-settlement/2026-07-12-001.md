- Finished the single SCCP ABI V1 shape across Rust, Solidity, Swift,
  JavaScript/TypeScript, Python, Kotlin, Java, C#, the diagnostic circuit, and
  release fixtures: eleven ordered public signals, twelve IC points, a 38-word
  verifying-key preimage, and mandatory typed `outbound_proof_policy`. The
  semantic-profile and Taira-finality-anchor values and hashes are bound into
  destination/deployment/route hashes, requests, readbacks, and role
  separation. Ten-signal, policy-less, diagnostic-circuit, Solana, and TON
  release representations have no compatibility decoder or fixture entry.
  Successful outbound messages now carry a dense execution-order
  `commitment_index` in `0..=511`; rejected execution cannot consume one. The
  global locator and ordered index retain that position while accepted proofs
  atomically replace pending payload-bearing records with fixed terminal replay
  descriptors.


<a id="record-3dc8a98f69ace0e335baa9019954424340944e903dee7516be2f1a52d3270bd3"></a>

- Made SCCP historical proof serving independent of block-body and mutable WSV
  payload retention. Before finality publication or body eviction, Kura writes
  an immutable retained canonical header plus root-authenticated SCCP archive;
  finality, message-bundle, proof-request, and recent-message reconstruction use
  that archive and fail closed on gaps, swaps, omissions, extras, root drift, or
  sidecar substitution. The fixed per-block/message limits are 512 and 4,096
  bytes. Pending WSV payloads are jointly bounded by the consensus-configured
  defaults of 65,536 messages and 268,435,456 bytes, and release their charge on
  the terminal transition. Permanent terminal replay/index state and immutable
  Kura history intentionally grow; sidecars count toward total/operator disk
  usage but not the evictable-body budget.


<a id="record-0cd8d30bf791d9bef7f0124eb7a5f2ed097fa897da6771546d4aa6fd9f4673a6"></a>

- Hardened the remaining SCCP state boundaries. Recording now requires the
  transaction `call_hash` before custody or outbox mutation, with snapshot
  equality tests proving the entire transaction state is unchanged on
  rejection. Signed snapshot admission validates the exact MV cell envelope,
  including both committed `blocks` and any non-null `revert` registry, and
  rejects missing or unknown cell fields, noncanonical ordering, duplicate
  lanes, and structurally canonical but off-curve BN254 keys before the state
  can become observable. Kura disk totals and cached query indexes now follow
  publication order across canonical rewrites, retired trees, lane geometry,
  journals, projections, rosters, and deferred cleanup; partial scan or cleanup
  failures invalidate affected totals instead of publishing stale accounting.

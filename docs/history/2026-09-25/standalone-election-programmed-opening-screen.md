# F11 research screen: noninteractive validation and programmed opening

Date: 2026-09-25. Read-only research on the existing `optimizations` checkout;
this record changes no protocol, key, or production guard. The binding
requirements are the [standalone election contract](../../../specs/standalone_election_protocol_contract.md)
and its equal-final-total privacy game. The fixed-slot zero/cast and old/latest
counterexamples are restated below.

## Result

No reviewed primary construction supplies the conjunction of (1) every finalized cast/update survives immediate voter dropout, (2) no decryption committee or master decryptor, and (3) an opening cryptographically limited to the unique final latest-version corpus. F11 remains unresolved. This is a **bounded literature result, not an impossibility theorem**. The nearest additional candidate found, PEAR/NIVA, contributes noninteractive validation of private inputs but explicitly retains a master secret key and permits arbitrary ciphertext-set aggregation; it is unsuitable as the standalone-election tally protocol.

## Exact fixed-slot test applied to the new candidates

Let two honest slots `A,B` have publicly retained zero and cast ciphertexts, with final unit-weight choices `(red, blue)` in world 0 and `(blue, red)` in world 1. Both finalized totals are `(1,1)`. Any key or decoder able to open the complete vector `(C_A, Z_B, ...)` distinguishes the worlds by revealing `A`'s singleton total, even if Core publishes only the valid final `(C_A, C_B, ...)` result. For an update, give `A` weight `1 -> 2` and `B` weight `2`: the final `(2,2)` agrees across swapped-choice worlds, but `(C_A^old, C_B^latest, ...)` yields `(1,2)` versus `(2,1)`. PEAR's `msk` trivially opens these choices; a reused decentralized sum key permits the same off-corpus evaluations. Repeated-query security does not protect two worlds whose available function outputs differ. A checker attached only to `FinalizeElection` cannot prevent off-chain evaluation using copied key material.

An exact-corpus-gated programmed opening remains a **research obligation**, not a selected protocol: a preissued capability would have to validate an unforgeable *unique* election-close certificate for the fixed network/election/policy, replay the authenticated ordered accepted history to its deterministic latest-state vector, and output only `T(H)`. It must reject a merely finalized prefix, alternative selection, fork, changed update predecessor, or extra close; no holder may extract a general decryptor or mint a different program. The construction and privacy proof must include every durable ciphertext/version, corruption and adaptive dropout transcript, worker traffic, errors and final-total leakage. The accepting cast/update must already contain all recovery material before its voter can disappear. Existing cited papers do not furnish this programmed capability under the no-master/no-committee rule.

Next proof obligation: instantiate `N,A,U,K,d,c`, network/worker assumptions and a finite close deadline; define the exact unique-close language and publicly verifiable latest-state relation; give a concrete setup/key-generation and opening algorithm with no privileged decryption capacity; prove equal-final-total simulation for all retained zero/cast/update ciphertexts and adaptive schedules; then independently review and measure maximum-size execution. Until that exists, the standalone production semantic guard and vote VK registry must stay closed.
